<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: BUSKING: STREET MUSICIANS IN DUBLIN</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">People who come to Dublin for the first time may be surprised by the huge number of street performers or buskers, especially</li>
  <li>on the tourist-packed Grafton Street. They have become part of Dublin’s cultural life.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Musicians from all over the world compete to be heard on Grafton Street, playing everything from authentic Irish folk to modern</li>
  <li>punk. Playing on Grafton Street isn’t like any other busking experience. It’s one of the busiest streets in Europe, with over 9,000</li>
  <li>pedestrians passing through every hour —each one could be receptive or rude, generous or quite the opposite.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The increased popularity of busking in Dublin has also brought with it legislation; performers pay for the privilege to play on</li>
  <li>Grafton Street (a permit costs €30 a year) and follow strict regulations. Each busker can only play for one hour before moving on</li>
  <li>and can’t return within 100 metres of any of their previous locations until the following day.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The musicians queue, sometimes for hours, for one of the best-designated spots. They’re also in direct competition with other</li>
  <li>buskers along the street playing at the same time, which ensures that everyone who performs here puts their full heart and soul</li>
  <li>into it.</li>
  <li class="is-start">A unique interrelation between buskers and fans occurs only in the streets, with no security staff, no VIP seats, or entrance</li>
  <li>fee. Christmas Eve, for instance, is a really good time to visit Grafton Street, as megastars like Bono and Glen Hansard have been</li>
  <li>known to turn up and play for charity. If you like what you’re hearing, leave a tip —€2 or €3 is typical, but more is obviously</li>
  <li>welcomed, especially if you stay for a while.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. According to the text, Grafton Street is the place where…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) buskers can be heard by many people.</span>
  <span>(b) car traffic is particularly heavy.</span>
  <span>(c) you can play for free.</span>
  <span>(d) only well-known musicians may perform.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(a) buskers can be heard by many people.</strong></p>
  <p>Es una de las calles más concurridas de Europa (líneas 4-5): <em>“one of the busiest streets in Europe, with over 9,000 pedestrians passing through every hour”</em>.</p>
  <p>Hablan de peatones, no de coches (b); para tocar hay que pagar un permiso de 30 € al año (c); y tocan músicos de todo el mundo, no solo famosos (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. In order to play on Grafton Street, buskers must…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) give Dublin City Council a winning percentage.</span>
  <span>(b) change spots every hour.</span>
  <span>(c) play Irish music.</span>
  <span>(d) pay every day they perform.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) change spots every hour.</strong></p>
  <p>Cada músico solo puede tocar una hora en el mismo sitio (línea 7): <em>“Each busker can only play for one hour before moving on”</em>.</p>
  <p>El texto no habla de dar un porcentaje al ayuntamiento (a); se toca de todo, del folk irlandés al punk (c); y el permiso se paga una vez al año, no cada día (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: A THOUSAND HUTS</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">Sustainability is a hot topic in the world of travel right now, but what does it actually mean? Everyone seems to have a different</li>
  <li>idea about what we should focus on. From electric cars and bicycles to slow food and zero-plastic hotels, there are countless</li>
  <li>factors that make a trip more sustainable. That’s where Scotland’s 1,000 Hut programme comes in. This initiative started in 2011,</li>
  <li>after a brainstorming session among Scottish environmental activists. Since then, they have been working with Reforesting</li>
  <li>Scotland to make cabins an alternative holiday destination, particularly for eco-conscious travellers who are fond of a minimalist</li>
  <li>holiday.</li>
  <li class="is-start">It could be a positive change in the way we holiday from now on, the initiative believes. Rather than trying to organise one</li>
  <li>large holiday a year in Spain or Greece, a family could find it easier to come to a smaller, closer rural Scottish hut whenever they</li>
  <li>need a break from the frenetic pace of urban life.</li>
  <li class="is-start">What the programme hopes is that “people will learn about and engage with nature, taking responsibility for the environment.”</li>
  <li>The experience includes travelling to the cabins in low-carbon, sustainable ways that could make visitors more appreciative of the</li>
  <li>world around them. Besides, it’s also a way of promoting local travel.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Increasing the number of simple, low-waste huts in the British countryside will also serve the larger number of people travelling</li>
  <li>within the UK. In this way, Scotland could position itself as a favourite closer destination. More people in rural communities means</li>
  <li>more money flowing into those communities, and the more people are enjoying the countryside, the more financially equipped we’ll</li>
  <li>be to preserve it.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. According to the text, sustainability…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) has a clear definition.</span>
  <span>(b) focuses on low-carbon transport.</span>
  <span>(c) is fashionable nowadays.</span>
  <span>(d) mainly involves eating green.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) is fashionable nowadays.</strong></p>
  <p>La sostenibilidad es un tema de actualidad en el mundo de los viajes (línea 1): <em>“Sustainability is a hot topic in the world of travel right now”</em>.</p>
  <p>Cada uno la entiende de una forma distinta (<em>“Everyone seems to have a different idea”</em>) (a); y el transporte o la comida son solo algunos de sus muchos factores (<em>“countless factors”</em>) (b y d).</p>
</Solucion>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) also offers urban accommodation.</span>
  <span>(b) has given rise to controversy.</span>
  <span>(c) was set up by nature campaigners.</span>
  <span>(d) originated in the Scottish Government.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) was set up by nature campaigners.</strong></p>
  <p>La iniciativa nació de una reunión de activistas medioambientales (línea 4): <em>“after a brainstorming session among Scottish environmental activists.”</em></p>
  <p>Las cabañas están en el campo, no en la ciudad (a); el texto no habla de polémica (b); y la crearon activistas, no el Gobierno escocés (d).</p>
</Solucion>
