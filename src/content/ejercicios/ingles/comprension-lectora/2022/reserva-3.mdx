<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: IT’S A GOOD DAY TO BE A DOG!</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">If you’re in the market for gourmet dog food these days, there are a lot of options for your puppy. I mean, if you’ve got bills to</li>
  <li>pay, you could buy a 10-kilo bag of chicken flavor for &#36;10. But the brand <em>Open Farm Pet</em> offers a similar size bag of “Norwegian</li>
  <li>Salmon &amp; Ancient Grains Dog Food” for &#36;72. It does contain not only wild salmon, but also quinoa, chia seeds and superfoods like</li>
  <li>coconut oil and pumpkin.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Lately, every element of pet culture has changed: from food to medicine, training, travel… Dogs have come a long way since</li>
  <li>the days when they were put to work on farms and given food scraps. From spending their nights in the doghouse to sleeping</li>
  <li>inside on comfortable beds, they’ve made big progress. They get first-rate healthcare and visits to psychiatrists who prescribe</li>
  <li>them antidepressants. They get massages and spa days, and wear sweaters.</li>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. According to the text, it is increasingly common for dogs to…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(b) use clothes.</span>
  <span>(c) get trained to perform work for their owners.</span>
  <span>(d) be fed exclusively on meat.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) use clothes.</strong></p>
  <p>Hoy los perros reciben masajes y hasta llevan jerséis (línea 8): <em>“They get massages and spa days, and wear sweaters.”</em></p>
  <p>Ahora duermen dentro de casa (<em>“sleeping inside on comfortable beds”</em>) (a); trabajar en el campo era cosa del pasado (c); y su comida lleva también quinoa, semillas o calabaza (d).</p>
</Solucion>

</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: WOMEN’S RIGHT TO VOTE IN THE UNITED KINGDOM</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">It is a fact that, throughout history, there have been severe restrictions regarding human rights based on age, race, education,</li>
  <li>social status and gender. During the early 20th century, men in the United Kingdom had the right to vote but women did not. Many</li>
  <li>people —including many women— believed that females should not be allowed to vote. However, a group of women started to</li>
  <li>campaign for women’s rights, especially the right to vote. This became known as the Suffragist Movement, with two groups: the</li>
  <li>Suffragists and the Suffragettes.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The Suffragists campaigned for votes for upper and middle-class women and believed in peaceful protest. The Suffragettes,</li>
  <li>in contrast, were frustrated with the Suffragists’ lack of results and created a more inclusive group in which women from all social</li>
  <li>classes and races were welcomed. They began using more confrontational tactics to get people to listen. Many protesting</li>
  <li>Suffragettes were arrested for law breaking and most of them went to prison.</li>
  <li class="is-start">In 1913, Suffragette Emily Davison stepped out in front of the King’s racehorse during a race event and died tragically after</li>
  <li>being trampled on. Emily’s death and the cruel police treatment led to public anger, and the female cause gathered support. After</li>
  <li>women’s active help during World War I, no one dared to neglect their right to vote, which was approved in Parliament in 1918.</li>
  <li class="is-start">There is some divided opinion over the tactics the Suffragettes used to spread their message, but they paved the way for future</li>
  <li>generations of women, and helped us all not only advance as a modern society but also make us aware of the importance of</li>
  <li>history in our lives.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. During the early 20<sup>th</sup> century, …</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) the entire population thought women shouldn’t vote.</span>
  <span>(b) only men disagreed in terms of women’s right to vote.</span>
  <span>(c) many citizens believed only men should vote.</span>
  <span>(d) only men and upper and middle-class women could vote.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) many citizens believed only men should vote.</strong></p>
  <p>Mucha gente, incluidas muchas mujeres, pensaba que ellas no debían votar (líneas 2-3): <em>“Many people —including many women— believed that females should not be allowed to vote.”</em></p>
  <p>No todos pensaban así, porque un grupo de mujeres luchó por el voto (a); también muchas mujeres se oponían (b); y entonces solo votaban los hombres (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(c) competed in a race event in England.</span>

</Solucion>
