<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: IT BEGAN 50 YEARS AGO</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">The ABBA story began in Sweden more than five decades ago. In 1966, Björn met Benny for the first time. Björn was a member</li>
  <li>of a well-known folk music group, while Benny played keyboards in Sweden’s biggest pop band of the 1960s. They wrote their first</li>
  <li>song just some weeks later, and by the end of the decade they had established a regular partnership as composers. In 1969, Björn</li>
  <li>and Benny met the two women who were to become not only their fiancées but also the other half of ABBA, Agnetha and Anni-Frid. In 1974,</li>
  <li>‘Waterloo’ took them straight to the Eurovision finals in England. By this time, they had changed their name to ABBA, an acronym of their</li>
  <li>first names. ABBA was also the brand of a Swedish canned fish company which agreed to lend their name to this pop group.</li>
  <li class="is-start">By 1976, ABBA had already established themselves as one of the most prevalent groups in the world. This was followed by a</li>
  <li>smashing concert tour of Europe and Australia between January and March 1977. At the end of 1982, after many worldwide hits and</li>
  <li>their respective divorces, ABBA decided to take a break. If they wanted to, they reasoned, they could always get back together after a few</li>
  <li>years. This “temporary break” resulted in more than thirty years of silence.</li>
  <li class="is-start">In 2010, the former couples authorized a touring exhibition and interactive experience called <em>ABBAWorld</em>, held in London,</li>
  <li>where they gave interviews and donated some personal items. In January 2016, a show-concept, <em>Mamma Mia</em>, opened in</li>
  <li>Stockholm. This was the first opportunity in three decades to capture the four members together exclusively in one and the same</li>
  <li>photograph.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. ABBA became famous…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) after their concert tour through Europe.</span>
  <span>(b) in the seventies.</span>
  <span>(c) when they first met in 1969.</span>
  <span>(d) few weeks after they had met.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) in the seventies.</strong></p>
  <p>En 1974 llegaron a Eurovisión y en 1976 ya eran uno de los grupos más importantes del mundo (línea 7): <em>“By 1976, ABBA had already established themselves as one of the most prevalent groups in the world.”</em></p>
  <p>La gira por Europa fue en 1977, cuando ya eran famosos (a); en 1969 solo se conocieron las dos parejas (c); y a las pocas semanas de conocerse solo escribieron su primera canción (d).</p>
</Solucion>

  <span>(b) announced that they would reunite.</span>
  <span>(c) agreed to pose for the media.</span>

<Solucion>

  <p>El texto no habla de un disco nuevo (a) ni de que anunciaran su vuelta (b); y donaron objetos personales en 2010, en la exposición de Londres (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: COLOUR PSYCHOLOGY</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">The psychology of colour is based on the mental and emotional effects colours have on sighted people in all facets of life.</li>
  <li>These reactions are rooted in cultural and personal experiences and are conditioned by the situational context. If you have ever</li>
  <li>felt uneasy in a yellow room, or relaxed and calm while looking at the sky, this will sound familiar.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Contrary to what one may think, little theoretical or empirical work has been conducted to date on colour’s influence on</li>
  <li>psychological functioning. Despite that, it has become a hot topic in marketing, art, design and other areas since researchers and</li>
  <li>experts have made a few important discoveries and observations about the effect colour has on moods, feelings and behaviours.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Additionally, colours are powerful tools of communication. For instance, colour preferences, from the clothes we wear to the</li>
  <li>car we drive, can sometimes make a statement about how we want other people to perceive us. Other factors such as age and</li>
  <li>gender can also influence the colour choices we make.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Besides, there is some proof that certain colours can even affect our performance. For example, one study has shown that</li>
  <li>exposing students to red colour before being examined impacts the test score negatively. More specifically, 71 college students</li>
  <li>were given a participant number before they took a five-minute test. The numbers were coloured either black, green or red, and</li>
  <li>the results proved that students who got red-coloured numbers scored approximately 20% lower than other participants.</li>
  <li class="is-start">However, this evidence is mostly anecdotal and colours can be subjective. Therefore, additional research is needed for us to</li>
  <li>have deeper comprehension of the different psychological effects of colour.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

  <span>(d) only affects certain aspects of life.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) is a very important issue in selling strategies.</strong></p>
  <p>Se ha convertido en un tema clave en el marketing (línea 5): <em>“it has become a hot topic in marketing, art, design and other areas”</em>.</p>
  <p>Influye en la ropa que llevamos (a) y la edad también afecta a nuestras preferencias (b); y actúa en todos los aspectos de la vida (<em>“in all facets of life”</em>) (d).</p>
</Solucion>
