<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: BOY GENIUS</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">In a lot of respects, 12-year-old Caleb Anderson is a normal Afro-American kid. But, when he’s not watching Netflix or playing with</li>
  <li>his action figures, he’s busy living life as a first-year university student. Caleb is currently studying at Chattahoochee Technical College</li>
  <li>in Georgia, USA, where he’ll graduate with a Degree in Aerospace Engineering in two years. “When I was like one year old, I wanted</li>
  <li>to go to space,” Caleb said, “I figured that aerospace engineering would be the best path. I’m not really intelligent, I just grasp</li>
  <li>information quickly. So, if I learn quicker, then I get ahead faster.”</li>
  <li class="is-start">His mother, Claire, first realized her son was gifted when he began imitating her speech at just four weeks old. When he was nine</li>
  <li>months old, Caleb could use more than 250 words in Sign Language. He was just 3 years old when he qualified for MENSA —a non-</li>
  <li>profit organization for people who score 98 out of 100 or higher in a standardized intelligence test. It’s essentially a club for exceptionally</li>
  <li>smart people.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The boy went on to complete high school by the age of 11 and now his parents supervise him on campus. But when it comes to helping</li>
  <li>with his homework, they cannot do much. “We are not rocket scientists,” his mother explained. Instead, “We have learnt that there are</li>
  <li>other things that we can teach him, mainly about principles such as compassion, kindness and looking for good in others.”</li>
  <li class="is-start">The family hopes that Caleb’s experience will help others without the same opportunities or resources in education, especially those</li>
  <li>belonging to racial minorities, like Caleb.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. Caleb’s parents…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) want him to be an engineer.</span>
  <span>(b) live on campus.</span>
  <span>(c) supervise his homework at university.</span>
  <span>(d) provide him with an education in values.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(d) provide him with an education in values.</strong></p>
  <p>Sus padres le enseñan valores (líneas 11-12): <em>“We have learnt that there are other things that we can teach him, mainly about principles such as compassion, kindness and looking for good in others.”</em></p>
  <p>Fue Caleb quien quiso estudiar ingeniería aeroespacial (a); sus padres lo supervisan en el campus, pero no se dice que vivan allí (b); y no pueden ayudarle mucho con los deberes (c).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. MENSA is a non-profit organization only for…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) gifted people.</span>
  <span>(b) talented black students.</span>
  <span>(c) parents with children at engineering institutions.</span>
  <span>(d) engineering students.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(a) gifted people.</strong></p>
  <p>MENSA es un club para personas excepcionalmente inteligentes (líneas 8-9): <em>“It’s essentially a club for exceptionally smart people.”</em></p>
  <p>No se limita a estudiantes negros (b), a padres (c) ni a estudiantes de ingeniería (d), sino a quien saque una puntuación muy alta en el test.</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: OMEGA-3</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">The difference between oily fish and white fish is like that between long distance runners and lazy people, and you may be surprised</li>
  <li>to learn that the fatty fish are the athletes here. Also known as “pelagic”, from the Greek word for ocean, because they are always on</li>
  <li>the move, sardines, tuna and their kind spend their entire lives swimming in the direction of the next meal. Such an exhausting existence</li>
  <li>requires a ready source of fuel, and the energy these fish need is saturated throughout their body tissues in the form of oil, ready to</li>
  <li>burn.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Studies on the benefits of this oil to human health started in the 70s. Then, Danish researchers studying Inuit populations in the</li>
  <li>Arctic regions noticed that, despite eating a diet high in saturated fat, there was low incidence of heart disease. They claimed this might</li>
  <li>be thanks to a fatty acid, omega-3, which is found almost exclusively in oily fish. It wasn’t long before a multibillion-dollar industry</li>
  <li>producing supplement pills was born.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Fish oil, we now know, is rich in the omega-3 fatty acids. They are a vital part of our cells and also play an important role in the</li>
  <li>production of hormones that control the thickness of the blood and the movement of artery walls. Fatty acids also regulate inflammation</li>
  <li>throughout the body. Yet, unlike other kinds of fat, our bodies cannot produce them and must take them from food. Some of these</li>
  <li>omega-3 acids are found in several vegetable sources such as nuts, seeds and leafy vegetables, while others are present in some</li>
  <li>animal fats, mainly in fish.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. According to the text, pelagic fish...</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) are only found in the Greek seas.</span>
  <span>(b) are always in search of food.</span>
  <span>(c) are white fish whose energy is unlimited.</span>
  <span>(d) lack saturated fat.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) are always in search of food.</strong></p>
  <p>Se pasan la vida nadando en busca de su próxima comida (línea 3): <em>“spend their entire lives swimming in the direction of the next meal.”</em></p>
  <p><em>Pelagic</em> viene de la palabra griega para “océano”, pero no viven solo en mares griegos (a); son peces azules, no blancos (c); y su cuerpo está lleno de grasa (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>10. Omega-3 acids...</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) are produced by the Inuits.</span>
  <span>(b) are only present in oily fish.</span>
  <span>(c) cannot be generated by the human organism.</span>
  <span>(d) cause heart disease.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) cannot be generated by the human organism.</strong></p>
  <p>Nuestro cuerpo no puede producirlos y debe tomarlos de los alimentos (línea 12): <em>“our bodies cannot produce them and must take them from food.”</em></p>
  <p>Los inuit los consumían en su dieta, no los producían (a); también están en algunos vegetales (b); y se asocian a menos enfermedades del corazón (d).</p>
</Solucion>
