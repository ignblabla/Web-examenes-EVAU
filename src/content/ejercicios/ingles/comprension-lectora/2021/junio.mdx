<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: IBERIAN LYNX COMES TO TOWN</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">The Iberian Lynx remains an endangered species, rarely seen by the general public and remaining only in a very few areas of the</li>
  <li>Spanish countryside, as efforts continue to protect it and increase the number of breeding pairs in the wild. The lynx generally hides</li>
  <li>away from humans and with very good reason, since eradication of its habitat, farming, roads and hunters are the main causes why</li>
  <li>this magnificent animal is endangered in the first place.</li>
  <li class="is-start">So, residents of Rociana del Condado in Huelva (near Doñana Park) were both surprised and excited when a young adult male was</li>
  <li>spotted running through the streets of the town on a Friday morning. News of its sighting spread rapidly and villagers gathered to try</li>
  <li>and capture a photograph of the lynx once it settled down by the church at the centre of the town.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Local police called technicians from El Acebuche Breeding Centre and agents from Seprona (Nature Protection Service), who managed</li>
  <li>to capture the animal two hours after it was first sighted. The young male appeared to be one year old and was not microchipped, which</li>
  <li>means that its birth had not been recorded as part of the programme to protect the species in the nearby Doñana Park.</li>
  <li class="is-start">After an initial check-up, the lynx was transferred to the Endangered Species Recovery Centre and will remain there until the final</li>
  <li>results of the tests, after which it can be tagged and released into the wild.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The organisations involved in saving the feline from extinction say that numbers are still very low, their aspiration being to achieve a</li>
  <li>move to the classification of “vulnerable” species rather than “endangered” within the next five years.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. According to the text, the lynx…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) faces extinction due to a combination of threats.</span>
  <span>(b) was hunted by local residents.</span>
  <span>(c) had escaped from the Endangered Species Recovery Centre.</span>
  <span>(d) got immediately chipped.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(a) faces extinction due to a combination of threats.</strong></p>
  <p>El lince está en peligro por varias causas a la vez (líneas 3-4): <em>“eradication of its habitat, farming, roads and hunters are the main causes why this magnificent animal is endangered in the first place.”</em></p>
  <p>Los vecinos no lo cazaron, sino que intentaron hacerle fotos (b); no tenía microchip, así que nunca había estado registrado en el programa de protección y no se había escapado de ningún centro (c); y no se le puso el chip de inmediato: se le podrá marcar cuando estén los resultados de las pruebas (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. According to the text, local police…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) were the first to spot the lynx in town.</span>
  <span>(b) were aided by Seprona agents.</span>
  <span>(c) chipped the animal.</span>
  <span>(d) took the animal to Doñana Park.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) were aided by Seprona agents.</strong></p>
  <p>La policía local llamó a técnicos del centro de cría y a agentes del Seprona, que lograron capturar al animal (líneas 8-9): <em>“Local police called technicians from El Acebuche Breeding Centre and agents from Seprona (Nature Protection Service), who managed to capture the animal”</em>.</p>
  <p>Fueron los vecinos quienes lo vieron primero (a); el lince no tenía chip y nadie se lo puso (c); y lo llevaron al Centro de Recuperación de Especies Amenazadas, no al Parque de Doñana (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: LITERATURE TO THE PEOPLE</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">The public plazas in Grenoble, France, might appear to be a prototypical European panorama of outdoor cafes, people walking, and</li>
  <li>picturesque historic architecture. But the city has recently installed a handful of vending machines that, rather than snacks, dispense</li>
  <li>short stories in an effort to change the way people kill time. Instead of checking Twitter or playing videogames, the city’s residents may</li>
  <li>discover a new story from the publisher <em>Short Édition</em>.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The vending machines can be used for free and operate 24 hours a day. Simply decide how long of a story you’d like (one, three, or</li>
  <li>five minutes), press the corresponding button, grab the printout and start reading. Currently, there are 600 short stories in the</li>
  <li>dispensers, which represent the best written works on <em>Short Édition</em> according to its community of 141,000 subscribers and 1,100</li>
  <li>authors.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Quentin Plepé, co-founder of <em>Short Édition</em>, and his team came up with the idea a couple of years ago as they were standing around</li>
  <li>a snack machine in their office. What if the same concept could be applied to literature, they wondered. After two years of development,</li>
  <li>they finally launched their design. “The city council got involved from the beginning. What they really liked was the fact that the</li>
  <li>dispensers distribute culture through the city in an original way,” Plepé says. “Stories are an important part of our life. More and more</li>
  <li>people don’t take the time anymore to sit and read a book. This is a way to have a little ‘bite’ of a story, just for a couple of minutes,”</li>
  <li>concludes Plepé.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. Short story dispensers have been installed in Grenoble to...</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) make people buy more books.</span>
  <span>(b) provide people with a free read.</span>
  <span>(c) make people buy more snacks.</span>
  <span>(d) encourage people to use social media.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) provide people with a free read.</strong></p>
  <p>Las máquinas dan relatos cortos y son gratuitas (línea 5): <em>“The vending machines can be used for free and operate 24 hours a day.”</em></p>
  <p>No venden libros (a) ni aperitivos, sino que dan relatos en lugar de aperitivos (<em>“rather than snacks, dispense short stories”</em>) (c); y buscan que la gente lea en vez de mirar Twitter (<em>“Instead of checking Twitter…”</em>) (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(c) try to change people’s habits in this city.</span>
  <span>(d) can only be found indoors.</span>

<Solucion>

  <p>Se instalaron hace poco (<em>“recently installed”</em>) (a); las diseñó el equipo de <em>Short Édition</em>, aunque el ayuntamiento participó desde el principio (b); y están en las plazas públicas, al aire libre (d).</p>
</Solucion>
