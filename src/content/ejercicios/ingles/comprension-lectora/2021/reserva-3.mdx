<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: NEW COMICS</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">Seeing your best friend in pain is never easy. Trinity Jagdeo knows that well, after her closest mate, Alexia, was diagnosed with a</li>
  <li>serious degenerative disease. Alexia had to spend months in hospital to finally understand that her life would never be the same again.</li>
  <li>Trinity did what she could to help her, but they could only watch films together. Eventually, she realized that Alexia needed somebody</li>
  <li>to look up to in order to help her through the pain she was suffering. It was at this point that Trinity started creating comics featuring</li>
  <li>superheroes with disabilities. It gave Trinity something to focus on, and it inspired her friend Alexia never to give up hope.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Disney ignored her suggestion to create characters with disabilities or special needs, so Trinity established her own non-profit</li>
  <li>organization —“From We Can’t to We Can”. Through that, she has written and illustrated comic books which prove that people can</li>
  <li>still be superheroes, even if they aren’t like everybody else. She has made three incredible comics. The characters in Trinity’s books</li>
  <li>are based on real people, and this only makes her work much more powerful. Alexia and other kids in her position are incredibly</li>
  <li>grateful for what Trinity has done. It’s no wonder that she gets asked to speak at schools and universities, because she’s just as</li>
  <li>inspiring as the characters in her stories.</li>
  <li class="is-start">This girl can definitely change the world, with or without superpowers. Hopefully, Trinity’s comics will convince mainstream networks</li>
  <li>and content creators to start giving a greater voice to those who still do not have one.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. Alexia knows that…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) her life has changed for ever.</span>
  <span>(b) she will get back to her old life one day.</span>
  <span>(c) she will spend her lifetime at the hospital.</span>
  <span>(d) she will become a famous heroine.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(a) her life has changed for ever.</strong></p>
  <p>Alexia comprendió que su vida nunca volvería a ser igual (línea 2): <em>“to finally understand that her life would never be the same again.”</em></p>
  <p>Es justo lo contrario: no volverá a su vida anterior (b); pasó meses en el hospital, no toda la vida (c); y el texto no dice que vaya a ser famosa (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. Trinity and Alexia...</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) used to spend all their time together reading comics.</span>
  <span>(b) give talks to inspire students.</span>
  <span>(c) created their first comic for Disney.</span>
  <span>(d) discovered the power of comics.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(d) discovered the power of comics.</strong></p>
  <p>Los cómics de Trinity dieron esperanza a Alexia (línea 5): <em>“It gave Trinity something to focus on, and it inspired her friend Alexia never to give up hope.”</em></p>
  <p>Antes solo podían ver películas juntas (a); quien da charlas es Trinity, no las dos (b); y Disney ignoró su propuesta (c).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: WHY EVERYONE SHOULD HAVE MUSIC IN THEIR LIVES</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">Most of us come across music almost every day: streaming on our mobile device, on the radio, television or in the streets. But why</li>
  <li>is it so important? According to Andy Smith, composer, musical director, educator and presenter on BBC, there are four main reasons</li>
  <li>why everyone should have music in their lives.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Firstly, music brings people together. It speaks to all when words can fail and, wherever you go in the world, it is understood. Music</li>
  <li>is a universal gift and its power to connect people is without question, as it is an art form with human interaction at its centre. Most</li>
  <li>importantly, music has the ability to join people through the instruments they play or through a shared interest in a particular style.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Secondly, music improves your health and well-being. It stimulates the brain, which in turn helps with pain relief, reducing stress. A</li>
  <li>study from Harvard University has shown that relaxing music may lower blood pressure and heart rate after physical exercise. It can</li>
  <li>also improve mood and reduce anxiety and, by connecting people, it can be an antidote to loneliness and social isolation.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Music can also improve resilience. There are many skills required in order to learn how to play a musical instrument: technique,</li>
  <li>reading music, expression, listening and, as you continue to perfect these skills, you gain self-assurance.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Finally, for Andy Smith, music is a creative means of expression for people from all social and cultural backgrounds to express</li>
  <li>themselves. Whether it is to transmit a political message, a particular emotion or just for pleasure, musicians have used sound and</li>
  <li>music since the dawn of humanity.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. According to the text, music…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) has been used since early times.</span>
  <span>(b) has never been used for political purposes.</span>
  <span>(c) has only been used for joy.</span>
  <span>(d) is an emotionless form of art.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(a) has been used since early times.</strong></p>
  <p>Los músicos han usado el sonido y la música desde el principio de la humanidad (líneas 13-14): <em>“musicians have used sound and music since the dawn of humanity.”</em></p>
  <p>También ha servido para transmitir mensajes políticos (b) y emociones, no solo por placer (c y d).</p>
</Solucion>
