<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO REDUCE DRIVING STRESS</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">I am testing a new car, trying to forget my troubles and concentrate on the driving, so that I do not become a danger to pedestrians.</li>
  <li>“Hello, Corinne, what’s stressing you right now?,” a calm voice speaks from my car’s audio system. The conversation that follows</li>
  <li>shows all the ways in which artificial intelligence could transform our experience behind the wheel: not by driving the car for us but by</li>
  <li>taking better care of us as we drive.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Three-quarters of U.S. workers drive to work alone. This may be a problem if we take into account that long trips to work are</li>
  <li>associated with serious health risks, such as high blood pressure or obesity. Technology, however, has long promised that the solution</li>
  <li>is right around the corner: self-driving cars. But it will likely be decades before such cars are a reality.</li>
  <li class="is-start">In the meantime, a small group of scientists are working on how cars can make us happier while we drive them. Their main project</li>
  <li>is this car: it is equipped with the latest technologies to decrease a driver’s stress. In fact, this car offers the option of practicing a</li>
  <li>guided breathing exercise while the driver’s seat vibrates at intervals, and then, there is a voice instructing the driver to breathe along</li>
  <li>with its rhythm.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The goal is a car that can detect a rise in a driver’s stress level, via seat and steering wheel sensors or neck-tension cameras. It</li>
  <li>would then automatically start the breathing exercise, talk about a problem or tell a joke to ease tension. So, after this experience, and</li>
  <li>while we wait for self-driving cars, I can’t wait for this vehicle to be on the market!</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. The writer might be a problem to pedestrians if…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) she didn’t know how to drive properly.</span>
  <span>(b) she weren’t paying attention.</span>
  <span>(c) the car didn’t stop talking.</span>
  <span>(d) the car were driving on its own.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) she weren’t paying attention.</strong></p>
  <p>Intenta concentrarse en la conducción para no ser un peligro para los peatones (línea 1): <em>“trying to forget my troubles and concentrate on the driving, so that I do not become a danger to pedestrians.”</em></p>
  <p>El peligro sería distraerse, no que no supiera conducir (a); la voz del coche está para ayudarla (c); y el coche no se conduce solo (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. The new car can…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) check the driver’s blood pressure.</span>
  <span>(b) self-drive.</span>
  <span>(c) find stress-free routes for us.</span>
  <span>(d) provide stress-relief strategies.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(d) provide stress-relief strategies.</strong></p>
  <p>El coche lleva tecnología para reducir el estrés del conductor, como ejercicios de respiración guiada (líneas 9-10): <em>“it is equipped with the latest technologies to decrease a driver’s stress.”</em></p>
  <p>La tensión alta aparece como riesgo de los trayectos largos, no como algo que mida el coche (a); no se conduce solo (b); y el texto no habla de buscar rutas (c).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: INTERGENERATIONAL CARE</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">Intergenerational care is defined as “planned ongoing activities that bring together different generations in shared settings to</li>
  <li>participate in experiences that are mutually beneficial.” The concept is thought to have originated during the 1970s in Tokyo when a</li>
  <li>nursery school and a care home merged together. The idea of joint settings quickly spread through the USA, Canada and the</li>
  <li>Netherlands. More recently, there has been a Channel 4 programme, “Old People’s Home for 4 Year Olds”, which has increased the</li>
  <li>awareness of the approach.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Supporters enthusiastically report that children benefit greatly as they get to see people of all ages and backgrounds. Some of the</li>
  <li>residents use wheelchairs, some have specialised equipment, so children get to understand that this is part of life and part of being</li>
  <li>older. Connecting with the elderly can also provide children with one-to-one reading opportunities which can boost their reading and</li>
  <li>vocabulary skills. Babies and young children can live miles away from their grandparents or older relatives, so visiting local residents</li>
  <li>provides them with the opportunity to mix with a generation that they may have never known.</li>
  <li class="is-start">But it is not only the children who benefit from the visits. The non-profit organisation “United for All Ages” reveals that the regular</li>
  <li>interactions help the elderly too. There are recognised improvements to their poor health, anxiety and loneliness. A nursery worker</li>
  <li>explains that “one lady hugs dolls as if they were her own children and our children interact with her with no judgement or opinions; it</li>
  <li>is all lovely to see.”</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. Intergenerational care benefits…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">
  <span>(a) are rare nowadays.</span>
  <span>(b) are enjoyed both by young and old people.</span>
  <span>(c) exclusively affect children.</span>
  <span>(d) have been observed for centuries.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) are enjoyed both by young and old people.</strong></p>
  <p>No solo se benefician los niños, también los mayores (líneas 11-12): <em>“But it is not only the children who benefit from the visits… the regular interactions help the elderly too.”</em></p>
  <p>La idea se ha extendido por varios países (a); los beneficios no son solo para los niños (c); y el concepto nació en los años 70, no hace siglos (d).</p>
</Solucion>

  <span>(c) are critical with the elderly.</span>
  <span>(d) cannot interact with the elderly.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>

</Solucion>
