<p class="en-heading">TEXT 1: THE PINK SAUCE DISASTER</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">If you logged onto TikTok or Twitter recently, you might have seen people talking about something called <em>Pink Sauce</em>. No, it’s</li>
  <li>not pasta made with cream and tomatoes, but a condiment made by a TikTok user named Chef Pii. Pii advertises Pink Sauce by</li>
  <li>dumping it on tacos, wings, egg rolls, salads and more. The bottle has an adorable design, and she’s shipping it everywhere. The</li>
  <li>problem? Almost everything.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Consumers are reporting all kinds of issues, ranging from misleading nutritional information to a spoiled smell. The chef posted</li>
  <li>videos of her preparing the sauce without gloves on. Once the negative reviews started pouring in, people realized that nobody</li>
  <li>knew what the actual ingredients were. In fact, Pink Sauce is unregulated and could likely make you sick. In a statement, Pii said</li>
  <li>“I guess when you’re great, you can’t make a mistake. My team’s working quickly to fix the issues.”</li>
  <li class="is-start">Pink Sauce is not the only food product created by a TikTok user which has gone viral for the wrong reasons. Earlier this year,</li>
  <li>someone called Deva Tillis posted a video complaining about a “spicy bowl” containing crawfish and eggs that she ordered from a</li>
  <li>small business on the platform. The bowl took nine days to reach her, and the fish was rotten by the time it arrived. The product</li>
  <li>was falsely advertised as being shipped with dry ice to preserve the seafood.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Small businesses selling unregulated food online isn’t new, according to an expert. “It’s not a TikTok phenomenon, but what’s</li>
  <li>different is that TikTok is really good at small consumption and making things go viral. However, just because it’s on TikTok, that</li>
  <li>doesn’t mean that it’s safe,” she concluded.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>1. According to the text, Pink Sauce…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(b) can accompany many dishes.</span>
  <span>(c) is thick tomato soup.</span>
  <span>(d) doesn’t have a very attractive packaging.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) can accompany many dishes.</strong></p>
  <p>La chef la anuncia echándola en todo tipo de platos (líneas 2-3): <em>“Pii advertises Pink Sauce by dumping it on tacos, wings, egg rolls, salads and more.”</em></p>
  <p>La envía a todas partes (<em>“she’s shipping it everywhere”</em>) (a); es un condimento, no una sopa ni pasta (c); y el bote tiene un diseño adorable (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-question"><strong>2. According to the text, customers…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(b) buy the product because it’s natural.</span>
  <span>(c) don’t know what the sauce is made of.</span>
  <span>(d) are posting videos to complain.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(c) don’t know what the sauce is made of.</strong></p>
  <p>Nadie sabía qué ingredientes llevaba la salsa (líneas 6-7): <em>“people realized that nobody knew what the actual ingredients were.”</em></p>
  <p>El texto no habla de devoluciones (a); la salsa no está regulada, no se dice que sea natural (b); y los vídeos de queja que menciona son de otro producto, el “spicy bowl” (d).</p>
</Solucion>

<p class="en-heading en-gap">TEXT 2: NO MORE MONDAYS</p>

<ol class="en-text">
  <li class="is-start">For some US schools, the pandemic created an opportunity to try new things. One of them was to make four-day school weeks</li>
  <li>the norm. As teachers’ salaries cannot be raised, shortening the school week is a bonus which has allowed administrators to retain</li>
  <li>and attract experienced teachers. Besides, a shorter school week may alleviate some of the workload on teachers and improve</li>
  <li>their mental health. Schools can also save money. However, since teachers are not receiving any pay cuts, savings are typically</li>
  <li>only between zero and three percent of the schools’ budget.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Currently, more than 1,600 schools across 24 US states have a four-day school week, and the trend is growing, giving students</li>
  <li>and teachers either Fridays or Mondays off. To make up for the time missed, the new model’s school day is, on average, about an</li>
  <li>hour longer than the traditional one.</li>
  <li class="is-start">Superintendents in districts which have transitioned to four-day weeks say that the vast majority of teachers, students,</li>
  <li>administrators and parents actually love the model, an assertion backed by research and surveys.</li>
  <li class="is-start">It is a fact that absenteeism and discipline have seen a marked improvement in most of these districts. As regards students’</li>
  <li>academic performance, results are varied. For example, students in Colorado had higher scores in Maths and English, whereas</li>
  <li>students in Oregon got poorer results. Still, graduation rates, particularly among Latino students, have increased since the model</li>
  <li>began.</li>
  <li class="is-start">The four-day week is still limited to small, rural schools in US states where travel distances are greater and hiring qualified</li>
  <li>educators is a challenge. Current research is giving rise to more innovative ways of schooling which work better for teachers,</li>
  <li>students and families.</li>
</ol>

<p class="en-task">CHOOSE AND WRITE THE CORRECT OPTION (A, B, C or D). (0.5 points each)</p>

<p class="en-question"><strong>9. According to the text, the four-day schedule has helped to…</strong></p>

<div class="en-options">

  <span>(b) keep and recruit teachers.</span>
  <span>(c) reduce the number of teachers.</span>
  <span>(d) improve student-teacher ratios.</span>
</div>

<Solucion>
  <p><strong>(b) keep and recruit teachers.</strong></p>
  <p>La semana de cuatro días ha servido para retener y atraer a profesores con experiencia (líneas 2-3): <em>“a bonus which has allowed administrators to retain and attract experienced teachers.”</em></p>
  <p>Los sueldos no se pueden subir (<em>“teachers’ salaries cannot be raised”</em>) (a); y el texto no habla de reducir la plantilla (c) ni de la ratio de alumnos por profesor (d).</p>
</Solucion>
