<p class="en-task"><strong>II * USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH A CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“The doctor convinced me… so much fat.”</strong> (not eat)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“When you get to the hotel, we… in the lobby.”</strong> (wait)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “The doctor convinced me <strong>not to eat</strong> so much fat.”</p>
    <p><em>Convince</em> + persona va seguido de <em>(not) to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “When you get to the hotel, we <strong>will be waiting</strong> in the lobby.”</p>
    <p>Se usa el futuro continuo para una acción que estará en curso en un momento del futuro (cuando llegues).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“Be careful, you… hurt yourself!”</strong> have to / should / might / must</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“They gave us the wrong room… mistake.”</strong> by / on / at / for</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “Be careful, you <strong>might</strong> hurt yourself!”</p>
    <p>Se avisa de una posibilidad, así que se usa el modal <em>might</em>.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “They gave us the wrong room <strong>by</strong> mistake.”</p>
    <p><em>By mistake</em> (“por error”) es una expresión fija.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THIS SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“Several foreign languages are taught in this school.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>We/They teach several foreign languages in this school.</strong></p>
    <p>Como la pasiva no indica quién enseña, se usa un sujeto genérico (<em>we</em> o <em>they</em>) y se mantiene el presente simple.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THIS SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“Today someone sent this interesting article to everyone in the group.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Today this interesting article was sent to everyone in the group.</strong> (o <strong>Today everyone in the group was sent this interesting article.</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>Send</em> tiene dos objetos, así que cualquiera puede ser el sujeto de la pasiva. El agente <em>someone</em> se omite.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“Nicole’s parents heard those rumours from <u>their neighbours</u>.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Who did Nicole’s parents hear those rumours from?</strong> (o <strong>From whom did Nicole’s parents hear those rumours?</strong>)</p>
    <p>Se pregunta por una persona con <em>who</em> (o <em>whom</em> detrás de la preposición), con el auxiliar <em>did</em>. La preposición <em>from</em> puede ir al final o delante de <em>whom</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“The new language assistant has a lovely Irish accent. I immediately took notice of it.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The new language assistant has a lovely Irish accent, which I immediately took notice of.</strong> (o <strong>…, of which I immediately took notice.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>it</em> se sustituye por <em>which</em>. La preposición <em>of</em> puede quedarse al final o ir delante de <em>which</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE NEXT SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“The night was so dark that we couldn’t see a thing.”</strong> It was…</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It was such a dark night that we couldn’t see a thing.</strong></p>
    <p><em>So</em> va delante de un adjetivo solo; <em>such</em> va delante de <em>a/an</em> + adjetivo + nombre.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THIS CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“We would have left the party...”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>We would have left the party if we had known the music was so bad.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p>Tercer condicional: si la principal lleva <em>would have</em> + participio, la condición va con <em>if</em> + sujeto + <em>had</em> + participio.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“I last saw him in 2019.”</strong> I haven’t...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I haven’t seen him since 2019 / for four years.</strong></p>
    <p><em>I last saw…</em> equivale al present perfect negativo con <em>since</em> + momento o <em>for</em> + periodo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THIS SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“Most people is used to check their email daily.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Most people <u>are</u> used to <u>checking</u> their email daily.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: <em>people</em> es plural. Segundo error: en <em>be used to</em>, <em>to</em> es preposición y va seguido de -ing.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO REPORTED SPEECH: <strong>“‘What was the weather like during your holiday abroad?’ Alison asked me.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Alison asked (me) what the weather was like / had been like during my/our holiday abroad.</strong></p>
    <p>Pregunta indirecta: orden de afirmativa (<em>what the weather was like</em>), el pasado puede retroceder a pasado perfecto y <em>your</em> pasa a <em>my/our</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>was</span><span>arrive</span><span>time</span><span>he</span><span>in</span><span>happy</span><span>appointment</span><span>to</span><span>the</span><span>for</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>He was happy to arrive in time for the appointment.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Be happy to</em> + infinitivo (“alegrarse de”), con la expresión <em>in time for</em> (“a tiempo para”).</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
