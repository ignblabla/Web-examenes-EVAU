<p class="en-task"><strong>USE OF ENGLISH (3 points; 0.5 points each)</strong>. CHOOSE OPTION 1 OR OPTION 2 AND ANSWER <strong><u>ALL</u></strong> THE QUESTIONS FROM THAT OPTION <strong><u>ONLY</u></strong>.</p>

<p class="en-option"><strong>OPTION 1</strong></p>

<p class="en-instruction">REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES WITHOUT CHANGING THE ORIGINAL MEANING. THE NEW SENTENCE MUST CLEARLY PRESENT A DIFFERENT LINGUISTIC STRUCTURE.</p>

<ul class="en-bullets en-bullets--disc">
  <li>BEGIN AS INDICATED OR USE THE WORD IN BRACKETS.</li>
  <li>THE WORDS PROVIDED MUST NOT BE CHANGED IN ANY WAY.</li>
</ul>

<div class="en-rewrite">
  <p><strong>B1. I’ve never driven such an uncomfortable car before.</strong><br /><strong>(LEAST)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>This is the least comfortable car I’ve ever driven.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Never… such an uncomfortable</em> se convierte en un superlativo de inferioridad: <em>the least</em> + adjetivo contrario (<em>comfortable</em>), con <em>ever</em> y el present perfect.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B2. He will go with you provided that you don’t drive fast.</strong><br /><strong>Unless…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Unless you drive (more) slowly, he will not go with you.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Provided that</em> (“siempre que”) pone una condición: irá si no conduces rápido. Con <em>unless</em> (“a menos que”) se expresa al revés: no irá a menos que conduzcas despacio. Por eso la principal pasa a negativa y <em>don’t drive fast</em> pasa a <em>drive (more) slowly</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B3. Although she is a good lawyer, she doesn’t always win her cases.</strong><br /><strong>Despite…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Despite (her) being a good lawyer, she doesn’t always win her cases.</strong> (o <strong>Despite the fact that she is a good lawyer…</strong> / <strong>Despite not always winning her cases, she is a good lawyer.</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>Although</em> va seguido de una oración; <em>despite</em> es una preposición y va seguido de -ing, de un nombre o de <em>the fact that</em> + oración.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B4. Claudia may know the right answer.</strong><br /><strong>(LIKELY)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It is likely that Claudia knows the right answer.</strong> (o <strong>Claudia is likely to know the right answer.</strong>)</p>
    <p>La posibilidad con <em>may</em> se expresa con <em>it is likely that</em> + oración, o con <em>be likely to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B5. Please don’t touch my laptop.</strong><br /><strong>I’d rather…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I’d rather you didn’t touch my laptop.</strong></p>
    <p>Para expresar lo que preferimos que haga otra persona se usa <em>would rather</em> + sujeto + pasado simple; el pasado se refiere al presente.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B6. Someone will take you to the train station in a fancy car.</strong><br /><strong>You…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>You will be taken to the train station in a fancy car.</strong></p>
    <p>Pasiva en futuro: <em>will be</em> + participio. El objeto <em>you</em> pasa a ser el sujeto y el agente genérico <em>someone</em> se omite.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>

<p class="en-option"><strong>OPTION 2</strong></p>

<p class="en-instruction">REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES WITHOUT CHANGING THE ORIGINAL MEANING. THE NEW SENTENCE MUST CLEARLY PRESENT A DIFFERENT LINGUISTIC STRUCTURE.</p>

<ul class="en-bullets en-bullets--disc">
  <li>BEGIN AS INDICATED OR USE THE WORD IN BRACKETS.</li>
  <li>THE WORDS PROVIDED MUST NOT BE CHANGED IN ANY WAY.</li>
</ul>

<div class="en-rewrite">
  <p><strong>B7. It was a cold evening, so we decided to stay indoors.</strong><br /><strong>(SUCH)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It was such a cold evening that we decided to stay indoors.</strong></p>
    <p>La consecuencia se expresa con <em>such</em> + <em>a/an</em> + adjetivo + nombre + <em>that</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B8. What time is the next bus to York?</strong><br /><strong>I would like to know…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I would like to know what time / when the next bus to York is / leaves.</strong></p>
    <p>Pregunta indirecta: tras <em>I would like to know</em>, la pregunta lleva el orden de una afirmativa, con el sujeto delante del verbo (<em>the next bus to York is</em>), y termina en punto, no en signo de interrogación.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B9. I gave her a watch, but it stopped working two days later.</strong><br /><strong>(WHICH)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I gave her a watch which stopped working two days later.</strong> (o <strong>The watch which I gave her stopped working two days later.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>it</em> se sustituye por el relativo <em>which</em> y desaparece <em>but</em>. En la segunda opción, <em>which</em> es objeto de la relativa.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B10. ‘Don’t leave the door unlocked,’ his mother told Peter.</strong><br /><strong>(WARNED)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>His mother warned Peter not to leave the door unlocked.</strong> (o <strong>Peter was warned (by his mother) not to leave the door unlocked.</strong>)</p>
    <p>Una orden negativa en estilo indirecto se construye con <em>warn</em> + persona + <em>not to</em> + infinitivo. También vale en pasiva, con Peter como sujeto.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B11. We’ll get to the theatre after the beginning of the play.</strong><br /><strong>By the time…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>By the time we get to the theatre, the play will have (already) begun.</strong></p>
    <p>Con <em>by the time</em> + presente simple (con valor de futuro), la acción que habrá terminado antes va en futuro perfecto: <em>will have begun</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B12. My sister ate a lot of sweets when she was a child.</strong><br /><strong>(USED)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>My sister used to eat a lot of sweets when she was a child.</strong> (o <strong>As a child, my sister used to eat a lot of sweets.</strong>)</p>
    <p>Un hábito del pasado que ya no se da se expresa con <em>used to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
