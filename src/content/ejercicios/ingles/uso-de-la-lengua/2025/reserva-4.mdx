<p class="en-task"><strong>USE OF ENGLISH (3 points; 0.5 points each)</strong>. CHOOSE OPTION 1 OR OPTION 2 AND ANSWER <strong><u>ALL</u></strong> THE QUESTIONS FROM THAT OPTION <strong><u>ONLY</u></strong>.</p>

<p class="en-option"><strong>OPTION 1</strong></p>

<p class="en-instruction">REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES WITHOUT CHANGING THE ORIGINAL MEANING. THE NEW SENTENCE MUST CLEARLY PRESENT A DIFFERENT LINGUISTIC STRUCTURE.</p>

<ul class="en-bullets en-bullets--disc">
  <li>BEGIN AS INDICATED OR USE THE WORD IN BRACKETS.</li>
  <li>THE WORDS PROVIDED MUST NOT BE CHANGED IN ANY WAY.</li>
</ul>

<div class="en-rewrite">
  <p><strong>B1. Adriana’s performance wasn’t as impressive as Julia’s.</strong><br /><strong>Julia’s performance…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Julia’s performance was more impressive than Adriana’s.</strong></p>
    <p>El comparativo de igualdad negativo (<em>not as… as</em>) se convierte en comparativo de superioridad (<em>more… than</em>), invirtiendo los términos.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B2. Mike is so unfit because he never does any exercise.</strong><br /><strong>If Mike…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>If Mike did (some) exercise, he wouldn’t be so unfit / he would be fit(ter).</strong></p>
    <p>Segundo condicional, para una situación presente imaginaria: <em>if</em> + pasado simple, <em>would</em> + infinitivo. Se imagina lo contrario de la realidad: <em>never does</em> → <em>did</em>, <em>is so unfit</em> → <em>wouldn’t be so unfit</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B3. I have arranged to have lunch with Joyce today.</strong><br /><strong>I am…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I am having (o going to have) lunch with Joyce today.</strong></p>
    <p>Para un plan ya acordado se usa el presente continuo con valor de futuro. También vale <em>be going to</em>, que expresa intención.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B4. The babysitter takes care of Tommy in the afternoon.</strong><br /><strong>(LOOKS)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The babysitter looks after Tommy in the afternoon.</strong></p>
    <p>El <em>phrasal verb</em> <em>look after</em> significa “cuidar de”, igual que <em>take care of</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B5. They were painting the house while we were away on holiday.</strong><br /><strong>(PAINTED)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The house was being painted while we were away on holiday.</strong> (o <strong>We were having/getting our house painted while we were away on holiday.</strong>)</p>
    <p>Pasiva del pasado continuo (<em>was being</em> + participio), con <em>the house</em> como sujeto. También vale la estructura causativa <em>have/get</em> + objeto + participio, porque fuimos nosotros quienes encargamos el trabajo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B6. You’ve been laughing at Sam’s little brother.</strong><br /><strong>The person whom…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The person whom you have been laughing at is / was Sam’s little brother.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Whom</em> es el relativo de persona en función de objeto; como <em>laugh at</em> lleva preposición, esta queda al final de la relativa. La oración principal se completa con <em>is/was Sam’s little brother</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>

<p class="en-option"><strong>OPTION 2</strong></p>

<p class="en-instruction">REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES WITHOUT CHANGING THE ORIGINAL MEANING. THE NEW SENTENCE MUST CLEARLY PRESENT A DIFFERENT LINGUISTIC STRUCTURE.</p>

<ul class="en-bullets en-bullets--disc">
  <li>BEGIN AS INDICATED OR USE THE WORD IN BRACKETS.</li>
  <li>THE WORDS PROVIDED MUST NOT BE CHANGED IN ANY WAY.</li>
</ul>

<div class="en-rewrite">
  <p><strong>B7. ‘I must hide Jane’s birthday present before she gets home,’ Karen said.</strong><br /><strong>(HAD)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Karen said that she had to hide Jane’s birthday present before she got home.</strong></p>
    <p>En estilo indirecto, <em>must</em> de obligación pasa a <em>had to</em>, el pronombre <em>I</em> a <em>she</em> y el presente <em>gets</em> retrocede a <em>got</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B8. John is an only child.</strong><br /><strong>John hasn’t…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>John hasn’t got any brothers (or sisters) / siblings.</strong></p>
    <p>Ser hijo único (<em>an only child</em>) significa no tener hermanos: <em>hasn’t got any</em> + plural.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B9. The microwave was so heavy that I needed help to carry it.</strong><br /><strong>It was…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It was such a heavy microwave that I needed help to carry it.</strong></p>
    <p><em>So</em> va delante de un adjetivo solo (<em>so heavy</em>); <em>such</em> va delante de <em>a/an</em> + adjetivo + nombre (<em>such a heavy microwave</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B10. The six o’clock train to Brighton couldn’t leave because there was a storm.</strong><br /><strong>(DUE)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The six o’clock train to Brighton couldn’t leave due to a storm.</strong> (o <strong>Due to a storm, the six o’clock train to Brighton couldn’t leave.</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>Because</em> va seguido de una oración; <em>due to</em> va seguido de un nombre (<em>a storm</em>), así que desaparece <em>there was</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B11. Perhaps they didn’t hear the good news last night.</strong><br /><strong>(MIGHT)</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>They might not have heard the good news last night.</strong></p>
    <p>Para una posibilidad sobre el pasado se usa <em>might (not) have</em> + participio, que sustituye a <em>perhaps</em> + pasado simple.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p><strong>B12. Houdini was believed to be a fabulous magician.</strong><br /><strong>People…</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>People believed that Houdini was a fabulous magician.</strong></p>
    <p>Se pasa de la pasiva con sujeto personal (<em>was believed to be</em>) a la activa con el sujeto genérico <em>people</em>: <em>people believed (that)</em> + oración con el verbo conjugado (<em>was</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
