<p class="en-task"><strong>II * USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH A CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“The lawyer agreed… the document.”</strong> (sign)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“The new talent show… before I got home.”</strong> (start)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “The lawyer agreed <strong>to sign</strong> the document.”</p>
    <p><em>Agree</em> va seguido de <em>to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “The new talent show <strong>had started</strong> before I got home.”</p>
    <p>Para una acción anterior a otra del pasado se usa el pasado perfecto.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“I am looking forward… seeing you next Monday.”</strong> at / to / in / by</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“My sister… her car fixed yesterday.”</strong> made / did / didn’t / had</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “I am looking forward <strong>to</strong> seeing you next Monday.”</p>
    <p><em>Look forward to</em> + -ing significa “tener ganas de”.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “My sister <strong>had</strong> her car fixed yesterday.”</p>
    <p>Estructura causativa <em>have</em> + objeto + participio: encargó el arreglo a otra persona.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“The crime had already been investigated.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The police (o They) had already investigated the crime.</strong></p>
    <p>Como la pasiva no indica quién investigó, se usa el agente lógico (<em>the police</em>) o <em>they</em>. Se mantiene el pasado perfecto.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“You should keep the boxes in the fridge.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The boxes should be kept in the fridge.</strong></p>
    <p>Con un modal, la pasiva se forma con modal + <em>be</em> + participio. El sujeto genérico <em>you</em> se omite.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“The pit where I fell was <u>2 metres</u> deep.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>How deep was the pit where you/I fell?</strong></p>
    <p>Para preguntar por la profundidad se usa <em>how deep</em>; con <em>be</em>, el verbo va delante del sujeto.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“Henry VIII married six times. He became king in 1509.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Henry VIII, who married six times, became king in 1509.</strong> (o <strong>Henry VIII, who became king in 1509, married six times.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>he</em> se sustituye por <em>who</em>. Como es un nombre propio, la relativa es explicativa y va entre comas.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“I wish I had gone by car.”</strong> I regret...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I regret not having gone by car.</strong> (o <strong>I regret not going by car.</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>Regret</em> + -ing expresa arrepentimiento por algo pasado. Como se lamenta no haberlo hecho, se usa la forma negativa <em>not having gone</em> o <em>not going</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“If they had taken the bus, …”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>If they had taken the bus, they would have arrived on time.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p>Tercer condicional: sujeto + <em>would/could/might (not) have</em> + participio. También vale un condicional mixto con consecuencia presente (<em>…, they would be here now</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“Although the exam was difficult, they passed.”</strong> In spite of...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>In spite of (the exam) being difficult, they passed (the exam).</strong> (o <strong>In spite of the fact (that) the exam was difficult, they passed.</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>In spite of</em> es una preposición y va seguido de -ing, de un nombre o de <em>the fact (that)</em> + oración.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THIS SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“The woman must had seen the crime because she was stood next to the thief.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The woman must <u>have</u> seen the crime because she was <u>standing</u> next to the thief.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: la deducción sobre el pasado es <em>must have</em> + participio. Segundo error: para una acción en curso en el pasado se usa el pasado continuo, <em>was standing</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO DIRECT SPEECH: <strong>“She asked me whether I had understood the film.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>“Did you understand the film?” she asked.</strong> (o <strong>“Have you understood the film?” she asked.</strong>)</p>
    <p>En estilo directo la pregunta recupera el orden interrogativo; el pasado perfecto vuelve a pasado simple o a present perfect, y <em>I</em> pasa a <em>you</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>up</span><span>ago</span><span>husband</span><span>years</span><span>gave</span><span>four</span><span>cigars</span><span>my</span><span>smoking</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>My husband gave up smoking cigars four years ago.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Give up</em> + -ing (“dejar de”), con la expresión de tiempo <em>four years ago</em> al final.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
