<p class="en-task"><strong>II * USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH A CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“Don’t worry, they are used to… in the rain.”</strong> (drive)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“When we were six, we… the same school.”</strong> (not attend)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “Don’t worry, they are used to <strong>driving</strong> in the rain.”</p>
    <p>En <em>be used to</em>, <em>to</em> es una preposición, así que va seguido de -ing.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “When we were six, we <strong>didn’t attend</strong> the same school.”</p>
    <p>Acción terminada en el pasado (<em>when we were six</em>): pasado simple negativo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“Keep flammable substances… from fire.”</strong> near / up / away / close</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“There’s… furniture in the room.”</strong> many / much / a lot / a few</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “Keep flammable substances <strong>away</strong> from fire.”</p>
    <p><em>Keep something away from</em> significa “mantener algo alejado de”.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “There’s <strong>much</strong> furniture in the room.”</p>
    <p><em>Furniture</em> es incontable, así que se descartan <em>many</em> y <em>a few</em>; <em>a lot</em> necesitaría <em>of</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“All children over the age of six have been required to wear a uniform at my school.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>They have required all children over the age of six to wear a uniform at my school.</strong></p>
    <p>Como la pasiva no indica quién lo exige, se usa el sujeto genérico <em>they</em>. Se mantiene el present perfect y la estructura <em>require somebody to do something</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“Someone is looking after the baby at day care.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The baby is being looked after at day care.</strong></p>
    <p>Pasiva del presente continuo (<em>is being</em> + participio). El <em>phrasal verb</em> <em>look after</em> mantiene la preposición detrás del participio.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“<u>On Monday</u>, the court took legal action against the criminal.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>When (o Which day / What day (of the week)) did the court take legal action against the criminal?</strong></p>
    <p>Se pregunta por el momento con <em>when</em> o <em>which/what day</em>. Como el verbo está en pasado simple, la pregunta lleva <em>did</em> + infinitivo (<em>take</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“The most important meeting in 2022 will be held in China. China is an overpopulated Asian country.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The most important meeting in 2022 will be held in China, which is an overpopulated Asian country.</strong> (o <strong>China, which is an overpopulated Asian country, will hold the most important meeting in 2022.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El segundo <em>China</em> se sustituye por <em>which</em>. Como es un nombre propio, la relativa es explicativa y va entre comas.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“Our new dishwasher was installed last Monday.”</strong> We had...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>We had our new dishwasher installed last Monday.</strong></p>
    <p>Estructura causativa <em>have</em> + objeto + participio: nosotros encargamos la instalación y la hizo otra persona.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“The planet can be saved if...”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The planet can be saved if we all reduce our carbon footprint.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p>Primer condicional: si la principal lleva <em>can</em> + infinitivo, la condición va con <em>if</em> + sujeto + presente simple.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“The armchair was so comfortable that he ordered two more at the shop.”</strong> It was such...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It was such a comfortable armchair that he ordered two more at the shop.</strong></p>
    <p><em>So</em> va delante de un adjetivo solo; <em>such</em> va delante de <em>a/an</em> + adjetivo + nombre.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THIS SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“I used to working for the government, but now my job is in the privately sector.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I used to <u>work</u> for the government, but now my job is in the <u>private</u> sector.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: <em>used to</em> (hábito pasado) va seguido de infinitivo. Segundo error: delante del nombre <em>sector</em> se necesita el adjetivo <em>private</em>, no el adverbio <em>privately</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO DIRECT SPEECH: <strong>“They announced that the cruise would continue its journey that week.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>“The cruise will continue its journey this week,” they announced.</strong></p>
    <p>En estilo directo, <em>would</em> vuelve a <em>will</em> y <em>that week</em> a <em>this week</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>news</span><span>we</span><span>is</span><span>have</span><span>the</span><span>chance</span><span>another</span><span>good</span><span>that</span><span>still</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The good news is that we still have another chance.</strong></p>
    <p><em>The good news is that</em> + oración (“lo bueno es que…”). <em>News</em> es incontable y va con el verbo en singular (<em>is</em>); <em>still</em> va delante del verbo <em>have</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
