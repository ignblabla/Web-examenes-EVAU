<p class="en-task"><strong>II * USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH A CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“Please, ask your brother… you with the washing-up.”</strong> (help)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“When she… up, she will be a veterinarian.”</strong> (grow)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “Please, ask your brother <strong>to help</strong> you with the washing-up.”</p>
    <p><em>Ask</em> + persona va seguido de <em>to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “When she <strong>grows</strong> up, she will be a veterinarian.”</p>
    <p>En las oraciones temporales con <em>when</em> referidas al futuro se usa el presente simple, no <em>will</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“Global warming is a problem we all need to sort... .”</strong> up / with / on / out</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“… cheese is there in the mouse trap?”</strong> How many / How far / How long / How much</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “Global warming is a problem we all need to sort <strong>out</strong>.”</p>
    <p>El <em>phrasal verb</em> <em>sort out</em> significa “resolver”.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “<strong>How much</strong> cheese is there in the mouse trap?”</p>
    <p><em>Cheese</em> es incontable, así que se pregunta por la cantidad con <em>how much</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“In a trade agreement, your word must be kept.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>(In a trade agreement,) you must keep your word (in a trade agreement).</strong></p>
    <p>Con un modal en pasiva (<em>must be kept</em>), la activa lleva modal + infinitivo, con el sujeto genérico <em>you</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“In democracy, all citizens should cast a vote in an election.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>In democracy, a vote should be cast in an election by all citizens.</strong></p>
    <p>Con un modal, la pasiva se forma con modal + <em>be</em> + participio (<em>cast</em> es irregular: <em>cast-cast-cast</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“We need to eat protein <u>because it is essential to build bones</u>.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Why do we/you need to eat protein?</strong></p>
    <p>Se pregunta por la causa con <em>why</em>, con el auxiliar <em>do</em> en presente simple.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“Cultivating your own vegetables is a healthy option. It is easier than it seems.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Cultivating your own vegetables, which is easier than it seems, is a healthy option.</strong> (o <strong>…, which is a healthy option, is easier than it seems.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>it</em> se sustituye por <em>which</em> en una relativa explicativa entre comas, que se refiere a toda la acción de cultivar.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“I will keep my promise if you keep yours.”</strong> As long as...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>As long as you keep your promise, I will keep mine.</strong></p>
    <p><em>As long as</em> (“siempre que”) introduce la condición en presente, como <em>if</em>. Los posesivos se ajustan al nuevo orden: <em>your promise</em> y <em>mine</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“If burglars had broken into the shop, ...”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>If burglars had broken into the shop, they would have stolen everything.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p>Tercer condicional: sujeto + <em>could(n’t)/would(n’t) have</em> + participio. También vale un condicional mixto (<em>…, we wouldn’t be open today</em>).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“They have worked in Thailand for two years.”</strong> They began...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>They began working (o to work) in Thailand two years ago.</strong></p>
    <p>El present perfect con <em>for</em> se convierte en el pasado simple de <em>begin</em> (+ -ing o <em>to</em>-infinitivo) con <em>ago</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THIS SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“Does she wants to come to the cinema with us the Sunday?”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Does she <u>want</u> to come to the cinema with us <u>on</u> Sunday?</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: tras el auxiliar <em>does</em> el verbo va en infinitivo, sin <em>-s</em>. Segundo error: con los días de la semana se usa <em>on</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO REPORTED SPEECH: <strong>“My brother cooked a very tasty potato omelette yesterday evening,” she said.</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>She said (that) her brother had cooked a very tasty potato omelette the previous evening / the evening before.</strong></p>
    <p>El pasado simple retrocede a pasado perfecto, <em>my</em> pasa a <em>her</em> y <em>yesterday evening</em> a <em>the previous evening</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>often</span><span>chores</span><span>parents</span><span>?</span><span>help</span><span>how</span><span>children</span><span>do</span><span>with</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>How often do children help parents with chores?</strong> (o <strong>How often do parents help children with chores?</strong>)</p>
    <p>Pregunta por la frecuencia con <em>how often</em> + <em>do</em> + sujeto + verbo. La estructura es <em>help somebody with something</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
