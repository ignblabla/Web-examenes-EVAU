<p class="en-task"><strong>II * USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH A CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“I don’t mind… up the dishes.”</strong> (wash)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“Mary was so angry that she demanded… the manager.”</strong> (see)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “I don’t mind <strong>washing</strong> up the dishes.”</p>
    <p>El verbo <em>mind</em> (“importar”) va seguido de -ing.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “Mary was so angry that she demanded <strong>to see</strong> the manager.”</p>
    <p>El verbo <em>demand</em> (“exigir”) va seguido de <em>to</em> + infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“Lunch will be ready… the time you get back.”</strong> on / of / in / by</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“It is… easier to ride a bicycle than explaining how it is ridden.”</strong> far / little / few / more</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “Lunch will be ready <strong>by</strong> the time you get back.”</p>
    <p><em>By the time</em> (“para cuando”) es una expresión fija.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “It is <strong>far</strong> easier to ride a bicycle than explaining how it is ridden.”</p>
    <p><em>Far</em> (como <em>much</em>) intensifica un comparativo: <em>far easier</em> (“mucho más fácil”). <em>More</em> no puede ir con <em>easier</em>, que ya es comparativo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“The Prime Minister was shouted at during her speech.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>People/They shouted at the Prime Minister during her speech.</strong></p>
    <p>Como la pasiva no indica quién gritó, en activa se usa un sujeto genérico (<em>people</em> o <em>they</em>). El verbo preposicional <em>shout at</em> mantiene la preposición delante de su objeto, y se conserva el pasado simple.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“They haven’t decided the exact time of the match yet.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The exact time of the match hasn’t been decided yet.</strong></p>
    <p>El objeto pasa a ser el sujeto, con el present perfect pasivo (<em>hasn’t been</em> + participio). El agente genérico <em>they</em> se omite y <em>yet</em> se mantiene al final.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“That car belongs to <u>my uncle</u>.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Who does that car belong to?</strong> (o <strong>To whom does that car belong?</strong>)</p>
    <p>Se pregunta por una persona con <em>who</em>. Como <em>belong to</em> lleva preposición, esta puede quedarse al final (<em>Who… belong to?</em>) o ir delante de <em>whom</em>, que es más formal. La pregunta en presente simple lleva <em>does</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“Helen is a friend. I went on holiday with her.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Helen is a friend (who/that) I went on holiday with.</strong> (o <strong>Helen is a friend with whom I went on holiday.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>her</em> se sustituye por un relativo de persona. La preposición <em>with</em> puede quedarse al final, y entonces el relativo puede omitirse, o ir delante del relativo, que entonces debe ser <em>whom</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“I don’t like being so tall.” I wish...</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I wish I wasn’t/weren’t so tall.</strong> (o <strong>I wish I was/were small(er)/short(er).</strong>)</p>
    <p>Para lamentar una situación presente se usa <em>wish</em> + pasado simple. Se desea lo contrario de la realidad: no ser tan alto, o ser más bajo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“I wouldn’t have got wet if...”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>I wouldn’t have got wet if I had taken an umbrella.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p>Es un tercer condicional, sobre una situación pasada que no ocurrió: si la oración principal lleva <em>would(n’t) have</em> + participio, la condición va con <em>if</em> + sujeto + <em>had(n’t)</em> + participio. Cualquier condición con sentido y con esa estructura es correcta.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“I’m sure Jane hasn’t left home yet.” Jane...</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Jane can’t have left home yet.</strong></p>
    <p>Para una deducción negativa casi segura sobre el pasado se usa <em>can’t have</em> + participio. Sustituye a <em>I’m sure</em> + oración negativa.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THIS SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“People is complaining about not be able to have their hair cut.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>People <u>are</u> complaining about not <u>being</u> able to have their hair cut.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: <em>people</em> es plural, así que el verbo es <em>are</em>. Segundo error: detrás de una preposición (<em>about</em>) el verbo va en -ing: <em>not being able</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO DIRECT SPEECH: <strong>“Alex told Amanda he would see her the following day.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>“I’ll see you tomorrow, Amanda,” Alex said.</strong></p>
    <p>Se pasa de estilo indirecto a directo: <em>would</em> vuelve a <em>will</em>, los pronombres cambian (<em>he</em> → <em>I</em>, <em>her</em> → <em>you</em>) y <em>the following day</em> vuelve a <em>tomorrow</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>correctly</span><span>students</span><span>of</span><span>the</span><span>none</span><span>question</span><span>the</span><span>answered</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>None of the students answered the question correctly.</strong></p>
    <p>El sujeto es <em>none of the students</em> (“ninguno de los estudiantes”). Después van el verbo, el objeto (<em>the question</em>) y, al final, el adverbio de modo <em>correctly</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
