<p class="en-task"><strong>USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“I … here for more than an hour and a half.”</strong> (wait)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“You had better … at least three times a week.”</strong> (exercise)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “I <strong>have been waiting</strong> (o <strong>have waited</strong>) here for more than an hour and a half.”</p>
    <p>Con <em>for</em> + duración hasta ahora se usa el present perfect; la forma continua subraya que la espera sigue.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “You had better <strong>exercise</strong> at least three times a week.”</p>
    <p><em>Had better</em> va seguido de infinitivo sin <em>to</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“As the office was closed, … could receive the parcel.”</strong> somebody / anybody / nobody / everybody</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“We need to concentrate … this project.”</strong> in / on / with / about</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “As the office was closed, <strong>nobody</strong> could receive the parcel.”</p>
    <p>Si la oficina estaba cerrada, nadie pudo recoger el paquete. Como el verbo (<em>could</em>) es afirmativo, la negación la aporta <em>nobody</em>.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “We need to concentrate <strong>on</strong> this project.”</p>
    <p><em>Concentrate on</em> (“concentrarse en”) lleva la preposición <em>on</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“The escaped criminal was seen at the train station yesterday by some passengers.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Some passengers saw the escaped criminal at the train station yesterday.</strong></p>
    <p>El agente (<em>by some passengers</em>) pasa a ser el sujeto y se mantiene el pasado simple: <em>was seen</em> → <em>saw</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“People say that Shakira is the best artist in South America.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It is said that Shakira is the best artist in South America.</strong> (o <strong>Shakira is said to be the best artist in South America.</strong>)</p>
    <p>Con verbos de opinión como <em>say</em> hay dos pasivas: impersonal (<em>it is said that</em> + oración) o con sujeto personal (<em>Shakira is said</em> + <em>to</em>-infinitivo).</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“Sonia used to drive to London <u>twice a month</u>.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>How often (o How many times) did Sonia use to drive to London?</strong></p>
    <p>Para preguntar por la frecuencia se usa <em>how often</em>. En interrogativa, <em>used to</em> se forma con <em>did</em> + <em>use to</em>, sin <em>-d</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“<em>Macbeth</em> is a powerful play. I read it last month.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong><em>Macbeth</em>, which I read last month, is a powerful play.</strong> (o <strong><em>Macbeth</em> is a powerful play that/which I read last month.</strong>)</p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>it</em> se sustituye por un relativo. Si la relativa sigue a <em>Macbeth</em> (nombre propio), es explicativa y va entre comas con <em>which</em>; si sigue a <em>a powerful play</em>, es especificativa.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“Everybody stayed indoors because the weather was cold.”</strong> Due to…</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Due to the cold weather, everybody stayed indoors.</strong> (o <strong>Due to the fact that the weather was cold…</strong> / <strong>Due to the weather being cold…</strong>)</p>
    <p><em>Because</em> va seguido de una oración; <em>due to</em> va seguido de un nombre, de <em>the fact that</em> + oración o de -ing.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONAL SENTENCE: <strong>“As long as we invest our money wisely, …”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>As long as we invest our money wisely, we will have a comfortable retirement.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p><em>As long as</em> (“siempre que”) funciona como un primer condicional: la oración principal lleva sujeto + <em>will/can/may</em> + infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“Every students must to call their parents right away.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Every <u>student</u> <u>must call</u> their parents right away.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: <em>every</em> va con un nombre en singular. Segundo error: el modal <em>must</em> va seguido de infinitivo sin <em>to</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO DIRECT SPEECH: <strong>“She told her friend that she wasn’t feeling well then.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>“I’m not feeling well right now,” she told her friend.</strong></p>
    <p>En estilo directo, el pasado continuo vuelve a presente continuo, <em>she</em> pasa a <em>I</em> y <em>then</em> a <em>(right) now</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO REPORTED SPEECH: <strong>“‘Shall I get you a drink?’ she asked me.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>She offered (to get) me a drink.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Shall I…?</em> es un ofrecimiento, así que en estilo indirecto se usa el verbo <em>offer</em>, seguido de <em>to</em>-infinitivo o directamente de los objetos.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>a</span><span>won’t</span><span>with</span><span>pity</span><span>it</span><span>travel</span><span>us</span><span>is</span><span>they</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It is a pity they won’t travel with us.</strong></p>
    <p>La expresión <em>it is a pity (that)</em> (“es una pena que”) va seguida de una oración; aquí se omite <em>that</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
