<p class="en-task"><strong>USE OF ENGLISH (3 points;</strong> 0.5 points each<strong>)</strong>. CHOOSE AND ANSWER <strong><u>ONLY 6</u></strong> (SIX) QUESTIONS.</p>

<div class="en-list en-questions">
  <p class="en-hanging">17. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT FORM OF THE VERB IN BRACKETS:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.1. <strong>“I would rather you … near me.”</strong> (not smoke)</p>
  <p class="en-sub">17.2. <strong>“I wish I … abroad last year.”</strong> (travel)</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>17.1.</strong> “I would rather you <strong>didn’t smoke</strong> near me.”</p>
    <p><em>Would rather</em> + otra persona + pasado simple expresa lo que preferimos que haga; el pasado se refiere al presente.</p>
    <p><strong>17.2.</strong> “I wish I <strong>had travelled</strong> (o <strong>traveled</strong>) abroad last year.”</p>
    <p>Para lamentar algo del pasado se usa <em>wish</em> + pasado perfecto.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">18. FILL IN THE GAPS WITH THE CORRECT OPTION:</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.1. <strong>“We suggested … for an hour.”</strong> gathering / to gather / to gathering / gather</p>
  <p class="en-sub">18.2. <strong>“I can’t remember the meaning of this word. I’ll look it … in the dictionary.”</strong> down / up / for / out</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>18.1.</strong> “We suggested <strong>gathering</strong> for an hour.”</p>
    <p>El verbo <em>suggest</em> va seguido de -ing (o de <em>that</em> + oración), nunca de <em>to</em>-infinitivo.</p>
    <p><strong>18.2.</strong> “I’ll look it <strong>up</strong> in the dictionary.”</p>
    <p>El <em>phrasal verb</em> <em>look up</em> significa “buscar” una palabra o un dato.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">19. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE ACTIVE VOICE: <strong>“The burgers are going to be prepared by Joseph.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Joseph is going to prepare the burgers.</strong></p>
    <p>El agente (<em>by Joseph</em>) pasa a ser el sujeto y se mantiene <em>be going to</em>, ahora en singular: <em>is going to prepare</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">20. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO THE PASSIVE VOICE: <strong>“Nobody has bought the decorations for the party yet.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The decorations for the party haven’t been bought yet.</strong></p>
    <p>Pasiva del present perfect (<em>have been</em> + participio). Como el sujeto <em>nobody</em> era negativo, la pasiva va en negativa.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">21. GIVE A QUESTION FOR THE UNDERLINED WORDS: <strong>“<u>The professor</u> told the students to remain silent.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Who told the students to remain silent?</strong></p>
    <p>Se pregunta por el sujeto, así que <em>who</em> ocupa su lugar y no se usa el auxiliar <em>did</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">22. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCES USING A RELATIVE. MAKE CHANGES IF NECESSARY: <strong>“The Chemistry teacher was late for her class that day. She is usually on time.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The Chemistry teacher, who is usually on time, was late for her class that day.</strong></p>
    <p>El pronombre <em>she</em> se sustituye por <em>who</em> en una relativa explicativa entre comas, porque solo añade información sobre una profesora ya identificada.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">23. REWRITE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING. BEGIN AS INDICATED: <strong>“They spent a week repainting the kitchen.”</strong> It took...</p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>It took them a week to repaint the kitchen.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Spend</em> + tiempo + -ing equivale a <em>it takes</em> + persona + tiempo + <em>to</em>-infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">24. COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE: <strong>“We’ll have to postpone the event so that…”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>We’ll have to postpone the event so that more people can attend.</strong> (por ejemplo)</p>
    <p><em>So that</em> expresa finalidad y va seguido de sujeto + presente simple, o sujeto + <em>can/may</em> + infinitivo.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">25. THERE ARE TWO MISTAKES IN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE. FIND THE MISTAKES AND REWRITE THE SENTENCE CORRECTLY: <strong>“When King Charles arrived to the British Museum, everyone were waiting at the door.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>When King Charles arrived <u>at</u> the British Museum, everyone <u>was</u> waiting at the door.</strong></p>
    <p>Primer error: con un edificio o lugar concreto se dice <em>arrive at</em>, no <em>arrive to</em>. Segundo error: <em>everyone</em> va con el verbo en singular.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">26. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO DIRECT SPEECH: <strong>“Grace apologized to Will for having forgotten his birthday.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>Grace said to / told Will, “I’m sorry I forgot (o have forgotten) your birthday.”</strong> (o <strong>Grace said, “Will, I’m sorry I forgot your birthday.”</strong>)</p>
    <p>Una disculpa en estilo indirecto (<em>apologized for</em> + -ing) se convierte en <em>I’m sorry</em> + oración; <em>his</em> pasa a <em>your</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">27. TURN THE FOLLOWING SENTENCE INTO REPORTED SPEECH: <strong>“‘Your exam will take place next week, Peter,’ the teacher said.”</strong></p>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>The teacher said to / told Peter that his exam would take place the following week.</strong></p>
    <p><em>Will</em> retrocede a <em>would</em>, <em>your</em> pasa a <em>his</em> y <em>next week</em> a <em>the following week</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>

  <p class="en-hanging">28. USE THE WORDS IN THE BOXES TO MAKE A MEANINGFUL SENTENCE. USE ALL AND ONLY THE WORDS IN THE BOXES WITHOUT CHANGING THEIR FORM:</p>
  <div class="en-words">
    <span>got</span><span>dress</span><span>my</span><span>Armani</span><span>wedding</span><span>designed</span><span>sister</span><span>by</span><span>her</span>
  </div>
  <Solucion>
    <p><strong>My sister got her wedding dress designed by Armani.</strong></p>
    <p>Estructura causativa <em>get</em> + objeto + participio, con el agente introducido por <em>by</em>.</p>
  </Solucion>
</div>
