Calcula $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} \left(2\operatorname{sen}^2(x) - \cos^2(x)\right)dx$.

<Solucion>

**Simplificamos el integrando.** Con $\cos^2(x) = 1 - \operatorname{sen}^2(x)$:

$$
2\operatorname{sen}^2(x) - \cos^2(x) = 3\operatorname{sen}^2(x) - 1
$$

**Primitiva de $\operatorname{sen}^2(x)$.** Con la fórmula del ángulo doble, $\operatorname{sen}^2(x) = \dfrac{1 - \cos(2x)}{2}$:

$$
\int \operatorname{sen}^2(x)\,dx = \int \frac{1 - \cos(2x)}{2}\,dx = \frac{x}{2} - \frac{\operatorname{sen}(2x)}{4} + C
$$

**Primitiva del integrando.**

$$
\int \left(3\operatorname{sen}^2(x) - 1\right)dx = \frac{3x}{2} - \frac{3\operatorname{sen}(2x)}{4} - x = \frac{x}{2} - \frac{3\operatorname{sen}(2x)}{4} + C
$$

**Regla de Barrow.** Con $\operatorname{sen}(\pi) = \operatorname{sen}(0) = 0$:

$$
\int_0^{\pi/2} \left(2\operatorname{sen}^2(x) - \cos^2(x)\right)dx = \left[\frac{x}{2} - \frac{3\operatorname{sen}(2x)}{4}\right]_0^{\pi/2} = \frac{\pi}{4} - 0 - 0 = \frac{\pi}{4}
$$

**Respuesta.** **$\dfrac{\pi}{4} \approx 0{,}79$**.

</Solucion>
