<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>People with disabilities are heroes in our world. Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>How important is music in your life? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>People with disabilities face many obstacles every day, and they deserve our admiration. However, I am not sure that calling them “heroes” is always right.</p>
  <p>On the one hand, many of them show incredible courage and strength. Paralympic athletes, for example, train extremely hard and achieve amazing results, and they are an inspiration for everyone.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, many people with disabilities simply want to live a normal life: to study, work and have fun like anyone else. Calling them heroes just for doing everyday things can make them feel different.</p>
  <p>In my opinion, what they really need is not to be admired, but a society without barriers, with accessible buildings, transport and jobs.</p>
  <p>Real equality is the best form of respect.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> En lugar de aceptar la afirmación sin más, la matiza con argumentos a favor y en contra, lo que demuestra pensamiento crítico. Termina con una propuesta concreta. Expresiones útiles: <em>face obstacles</em>, <em>deserve</em>, <em>an inspiration for</em>, <em>like anyone else</em>, <em>not… but…</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Music is extremely important in my life. I cannot imagine a single day without it.</p>
  <p>I listen to music almost all the time: when I wake up, on the bus, while I study and before going to sleep. Depending on my mood, I choose different styles. When I need energy, I listen to rock, and when I want to relax, I prefer acoustic music.</p>
  <p>Music also helps me with my emotions. When I am sad or stressed, my favourite songs make me feel better, and some songs bring back wonderful memories of summer holidays with my friends.</p>
  <p>Moreover, I have been playing the guitar for four years, and playing with my friends is one of my favourite hobbies.</p>
  <p>In short, music is my constant companion.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde con rotundidad y lo demuestra con ejemplos de su rutina, de sus emociones y de su afición. Expresiones útiles: <em>I cannot imagine… without</em>, <em>depending on my mood</em>, <em>bring back memories</em>, el present perfect continuous (<em>I have been playing… for four years</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>
