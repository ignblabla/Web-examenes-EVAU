<p class="en-title" lang="es">El examen consta de 3 Bloques (A, B y C)</p>

<p lang="es">En cada bloque (<strong>Comprehension</strong>, <strong>Use of English</strong> y <strong>Writing</strong>) se plantean varias preguntas, de las cuales se deberá responder al número que se indica en cada uno. En caso de responder más cuestiones de las requeridas, serán tenidas en cuenta las respondidas en primer lugar hasta alcanzar dicho número. Las preguntas han de ser respondidas en su totalidad: si la pregunta tiene dos secciones, hay que responder ambas.</p>

<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Animal rights are important. Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Do you prefer reading or watching a film? What kind of stories do you like best? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>I strongly believe that animal rights are important, because animals can feel pain and fear, just like us.</p>
  <p>Firstly, it is our responsibility to protect animals from cruelty. Unfortunately, many pets are abandoned every year, and some farm animals live in terrible conditions. Laws should be stricter to prevent this.</p>
  <p>Secondly, protecting animals also means protecting nature. Many species are in danger of extinction because of hunting, pollution and the destruction of their habitats, like the Iberian lynx in Andalusia.</p>
  <p>Some people say that human problems are more important. However, respecting animals does not mean forgetting about people. We can and should care about both at the same time.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, a society that respects animals is a more civilised society.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Postura clara desde la primera frase, dos argumentos con ejemplos (el abandono, el lince ibérico) y una respuesta a la objeción contraria, todo en unas 120 palabras. Expresiones útiles: <em>I strongly believe</em>, <em>protect from</em>, <em>in danger of extinction</em>, <em>does not mean</em> + -ing.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I enjoy both, but I prefer reading a book to watching a film.</p>
  <p>The main reason is that books let me use my imagination. When I read, I create the characters and places in my mind, and the story feels more personal. Besides, books usually include more details and thoughts of the characters than films.</p>
  <p>However, films are great when I am tired or when I want to share the experience with friends.</p>
  <p>As for the kind of stories, I like mystery and fantasy best. I love trying to guess who the murderer is in a detective novel, and fantasy takes me to magical worlds far from everyday life.</p>
  <p>In short, a good story is always a good plan, whether on paper or on screen.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La pregunta tiene dos partes y la redacción responde a las dos: primero la preferencia (con sus razones) y después el tipo de historias (<em>As for…</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>prefer</em> + -ing <em>to</em> + -ing, <em>let me use</em>, <em>far from</em>, <em>whether… or…</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
