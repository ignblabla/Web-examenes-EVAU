<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>If you had a superpower, what would it be and what would you do? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Are your eating habits healthy? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>If I had a superpower, I would choose the ability to teleport, that is, to travel anywhere in the world in a second.</p>
  <p>First of all, I would use it to visit my family more often. Some of my relatives live in Argentina, and I would be able to have lunch with them every Sunday.</p>
  <p>Secondly, I would travel around the world without spending money or time on planes. I could have breakfast in Paris, see the pyramids in the afternoon and watch the sunset in Bali.</p>
  <p>I would also use my power to help others, for example by taking doctors and food to places affected by natural disasters.</p>
  <p>Best of all, I would never be late for school again!</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde a las dos preguntas (qué poder y qué haría) usando el segundo condicional en todo el texto (<em>If I had…, I would…</em>, <em>I could</em>, <em>I would be able to</em>). El final con humor es un buen recurso si encaja con el tema. Expresiones útiles: <em>that is</em>, <em>be affected by</em>, <em>best of all</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I think my eating habits are quite healthy, although there are some things I should improve.</p>
  <p>On the positive side, I follow the Mediterranean diet at home. My parents cook every day, so we eat a lot of vegetables, fish, pulses and olive oil. I also have fruit for breakfast and I drink plenty of water.</p>
  <p>On the negative side, I sometimes eat too many snacks, especially when I am studying for exams. Crisps and chocolate are my weakness. Besides, at weekends I often eat fast food with my friends.</p>
  <p>To improve my habits, I am going to replace snacks with nuts or fruit and limit fast food to once a month.</p>
  <p>In short, I eat well, but I can do better.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Hace una valoración honesta con lo positivo y lo negativo de sus hábitos y termina con un plan concreto (<em>I am going to…</em>). Vocabulario útil: <em>the Mediterranean diet</em>, <em>pulses</em>, <em>plenty of</em>, <em>crisps</em>, <em>my weakness</em>, <em>replace… with…</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
