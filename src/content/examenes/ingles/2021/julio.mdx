<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Do you think that young people prefer living in the city to living in the countryside? Why?</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>All children should grow up with a pet. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>In my opinion, most young people prefer living in the city to living in the countryside, mainly for practical reasons.</p>
  <p>Firstly, cities offer more opportunities. Universities, jobs and training courses are usually in big cities, so many young people have to move there after school.</p>
  <p>Secondly, there is much more to do. Cities have cinemas, concerts, shopping centres and a lively nightlife, while villages can be boring for teenagers, especially in winter.</p>
  <p>However, some young people value the peace, the nature and the strong sense of community of rural areas. Since the pandemic, thanks to working from home, some have even moved back to their villages.</p>
  <p>To sum up, the city is still more attractive for most young people, although the countryside has many advantages.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde al <em>Why?</em> con dos razones (oportunidades y ocio) y matiza con quienes prefieren el campo. Expresiones útiles: <em>prefer</em> + -ing <em>to</em> + -ing, <em>there is much more to do</em>, <em>a lively nightlife</em>, <em>while</em> para contrastar, <em>a sense of community</em>, <em>move back</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I agree that growing up with a pet is very positive for children, although it is not always possible.</p>
  <p>Firstly, pets teach children responsibility. Feeding a dog, taking it for a walk or cleaning a cat’s litter tray every day helps them understand that animals depend on them.</p>
  <p>Secondly, pets are wonderful companions. They give unconditional love, and many children talk to their pets when they are sad. Some studies also show that children who grow up with animals are more active.</p>
  <p>However, a pet needs time, space and money. Not all families can offer this, and an animal should never be a toy that is abandoned after a few months.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, pets are great for children, but only if the whole family is ready to look after them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Muestra su acuerdo con un matiz, da dos argumentos (responsabilidad y compañía) y expone las condiciones necesarias, lo que evita una respuesta simplista. Expresiones útiles: <em>take it for a walk</em>, <em>depend on</em>, <em>unconditional love</em>, <em>only if</em>, <em>be ready to</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
