<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Eating out or eating at home? Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What would you change to have a better world? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Both eating out and eating at home have advantages, so the choice depends on the situation.</p>
  <p>Eating out is ideal for special occasions. It is relaxing, because nobody has to cook or clean, and it allows us to try new dishes and cuisines from other countries. It is also a nice way to meet friends.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, eating at home is cheaper and usually healthier, since we can choose fresh ingredients and control what we eat. Moreover, family meals at home are a good moment to talk and share our day.</p>
  <p>Personally, I prefer eating at home during the week and going to a restaurant once in a while, to celebrate something.</p>
  <p>In my opinion, that is the perfect balance.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Compara las dos opciones, una por párrafo, y termina con su preferencia personal. Expresiones útiles: <em>depend on</em>, <em>allow us to</em>, <em>since</em> para expresar causa, <em>share our day</em>, <em>once in a while</em>, <em>the perfect balance</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>If I could change some things to have a better world, I would start with three.</p>
  <p>First, I would end poverty and hunger. It is unacceptable that millions of people do not have enough food or clean water while others waste so much. I would make sure everyone had access to basic needs, education and healthcare.</p>
  <p>Second, I would protect the environment. I would replace fossil fuels with renewable energy and ban single-use plastics, so that future generations could enjoy a healthy planet.</p>
  <p>Finally, I would promote tolerance. Many conflicts start because people do not accept those who are different, so I would teach respect and empathy at school from an early age.</p>
  <p>The world will not change overnight, but each of us can contribute a little.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Propone tres cambios (pobreza, medio ambiente, tolerancia) con el condicional (<em>I would…</em>) y los justifica. La conclusión vuelve a la realidad con un mensaje positivo. Expresiones útiles: <em>it is unacceptable that</em>, <em>make sure</em>, <em>replace… with…</em>, <em>so that</em>, <em>from an early age</em>, <em>overnight</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
