<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF APPROXIMATELY 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS STRICTLY ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Apart from cars, how could technology and artificial intelligence improve our lives? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Older generations can teach us useful lessons. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Self-driving cars are the most famous example, but technology and artificial intelligence could improve our lives in many other ways.</p>
  <p>In medicine, AI can analyse X-rays and medical tests very quickly, helping doctors detect diseases such as cancer earlier and save more lives.</p>
  <p>In education, intelligent programs could adapt lessons to each student’s level and rhythm, so nobody would be left behind.</p>
  <p>At home, smart devices could help elderly people live independently, for example by reminding them to take their medicine or calling for help if they fall.</p>
  <p>Finally, technology could help us fight climate change by controlling energy consumption in buildings and cities.</p>
  <p>Of course, we must use it responsibly, protect our privacy and make sure that it helps everyone, not just a few.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Deja de lado los coches, como pide el enunciado (<em>Apart from cars</em>), y da cuatro ámbitos con un ejemplo cada uno: medicina, educación, hogar y medio ambiente. Expresiones útiles: <em>could improve</em>, <em>be left behind</em>, <em>remind someone to</em>, <em>by</em> + -ing.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I agree that older generations can teach us very useful lessons, and we should listen to them more.</p>
  <p>Firstly, they have lived through difficult times, such as poverty or dictatorship, so they know the value of things we take for granted, like freedom, education or having enough food.</p>
  <p>Secondly, they have practical knowledge that is being lost. My grandmother, for example, knows how to cook traditional recipes, sew and grow vegetables, and she has taught me some of these skills.</p>
  <p>Moreover, they can give us good advice about life, relationships and work, because they have already made many mistakes.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, spending time with older people is not only kind, but also a great opportunity to learn from their experience.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Muestra su acuerdo y da tres tipos de lecciones (valores, habilidades prácticas y consejos), con un ejemplo familiar. Expresiones útiles: <em>live through</em>, <em>take for granted</em>, <em>is being lost</em> (pasiva continua), <em>give advice</em>, <em>not only… but also</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
