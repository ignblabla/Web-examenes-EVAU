<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>What type of films or series do you like best? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Pros and cons of sharing your life on social media. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>I enjoy many kinds of films and series, but my favourites are science fiction stories.</p>
  <p>The first reason is that they make me use my imagination. Science fiction shows worlds that do not exist yet, with space travel, robots or advanced technology, and I love imagining what life could be like in the future.</p>
  <p>Another reason is that the best science fiction makes you think. Behind the special effects, many of these films deal with important questions, such as the dangers of technology or what it means to be human. After watching one, I often discuss it with my friends for hours.</p>
  <p>Finally, they are usually full of action and surprises, so I never get bored.</p>
  <p>All in all, science fiction combines entertainment and reflection, and that is why it is the genre I like best.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Nombra el género en la primera frase y da tres razones ordenadas (<em>the first reason</em>, <em>another reason</em>, <em>finally</em>), cada una con una explicación. Expresiones útiles: <em>what life could be like</em>, <em>deal with</em>, <em>such as</em>, <em>what it means to be</em>, <em>never get bored</em>, <em>that is why</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Nowadays, many people share almost every moment of their lives on social media. This has both advantages and disadvantages.</p>
  <p>On the one hand, sharing your life online helps you keep in touch with friends and relatives who live far away. It is also a way of expressing yourself and showing your interests, and some people even turn it into a job.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, there are important risks. Once something is posted, it is very difficult to delete it completely, and a photo or comment can cause problems years later, for example when looking for a job. Furthermore, sharing too much personal information can be dangerous, since strangers may find out where you live or study.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, social media can be positive if we think twice before posting and keep some parts of our lives private.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Para un tema de <em>pros and cons</em>, la estructura ideal es un párrafo para cada lado (<em>On the one hand… On the other hand…</em>) y una conclusión con una recomendación. Expresiones útiles: <em>keep in touch with</em>, <em>turn it into</em>, <em>once something is posted</em>, <em>find out</em>, <em>think twice</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
