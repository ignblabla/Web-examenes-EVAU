<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Which places in the world do you find fascinating? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What makes a good friend for you? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>There are many places in the world that I find fascinating, but two of them stand out: Iceland and Egypt.</p>
  <p>Iceland fascinates me because of its incredible nature. It has volcanoes, glaciers, waterfalls and hot springs, and in winter you can see the Northern Lights. It seems like a completely different planet, and I would love to drive around the island one day.</p>
  <p>Egypt, on the other hand, attracts me because of its history. I have always been interested in ancient civilisations, and the pyramids and the temples along the Nile are thousands of years old. It is amazing to think how they were built without modern technology.</p>
  <p>Both places are very different, but they have something in common: they make you feel small in front of nature and history.</p>
  <p>I hope I can visit them in the future.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Elige dos lugares y dedica un párrafo a cada uno, explicando qué le fascina (la naturaleza y la historia). La conclusión busca lo que tienen en común. Expresiones útiles: <em>stand out</em>, <em>because of</em>, <em>it seems like</em>, <em>on the other hand</em>, <em>have something in common</em> y la pasiva <em>how they were built</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>For me, a good friend is not someone you just have fun with, but someone you can count on in good and bad times.</p>
  <p>First of all, a good friend must be loyal. If I tell a friend a secret, I need to know that they will not tell anyone else. Loyalty also means defending your friends when others criticise them.</p>
  <p>Secondly, a good friend is honest. Sometimes it is easier to say what the other person wants to hear, but a real friend tells you the truth, even if it hurts, because they want the best for you.</p>
  <p>Finally, a good friend is there when you need them. Last year, when I was going through a difficult time, my best friend called me every day and helped me with my studies.</p>
  <p>In short, loyalty, honesty and support are the qualities I value most in a friend.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Define la idea en la introducción y la desarrolla con tres cualidades, una por párrafo, y un ejemplo personal en la última. Expresiones útiles: <em>count on someone</em>, <em>even if it hurts</em>, <em>be there for someone</em>, <em>go through a difficult time</em>, <em>the qualities I value most</em>. Fíjate en el uso de <em>they</em> para no especificar el género.</p>
</Redaccion>
