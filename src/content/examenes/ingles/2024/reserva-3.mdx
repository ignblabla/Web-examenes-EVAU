<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Are you a day or night person? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What type of outdoor activities do you enjoy best? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Some people are full of energy as soon as they wake up, while others only come to life at night. I am definitely a night person.</p>
  <p>Getting up early is a real challenge for me. On school days my alarm goes off at seven, and during the first hour I find it very hard to concentrate. I usually have breakfast in silence and do not want to talk to anyone.</p>
  <p>However, in the evening I feel much more active. I prefer to study after dinner, when the house is quiet and nobody interrupts me, and I can work for hours without getting tired. It is also when I feel more creative, so I often write or listen to music late at night.</p>
  <p>The problem is that I sometimes go to bed too late. That is why I am trying to change my routine during the exam period.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde en la introducción y lo explica con ejemplos de su rutina diaria, contrastando la mañana y la noche (<em>However, in the evening…</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>come to life</em>, <em>my alarm goes off</em>, <em>find it hard to</em>, <em>without getting tired</em> y el presente continuo para un cambio en curso (<em>I am trying to change</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I love spending time outdoors, and the activity I enjoy most is hiking in the mountains.</p>
  <p>The main reason is that it makes me feel free. When I am walking through a forest or reaching the top of a mountain, I forget about exams and problems, and I can finally disconnect from my phone.</p>
  <p>Another reason is that it is a healthy activity. Hiking is good exercise for the whole body, and breathing fresh air is much better than spending the weekend at home in front of a screen.</p>
  <p>Besides, I usually go hiking with my family or with friends, so it is also a great way of spending time together. We take sandwiches, stop to enjoy the views and talk a lot.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, hiking combines nature, exercise and good company, which is why it is my favourite outdoor activity.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Da una respuesta concreta y tres razones (libertad, salud, compañía), cada una con detalles personales. Expresiones útiles: <em>the activity I enjoy most</em>, <em>forget about</em>, <em>disconnect from</em>, <em>much better than</em>, <em>a great way of</em> + -ing, <em>which is why</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
