<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>“There should be more money spent on scientific research.” Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>The importance of personal care and beauty today. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>I completely agree that more money should be spent on scientific research, as it is the key to solving many of our problems.</p>
  <p>Firstly, research saves lives. The COVID-19 vaccines were developed in record time because scientists had been working on similar technologies for years. More investment could help us find treatments for diseases such as cancer or Alzheimer’s.</p>
  <p>Secondly, science is essential to fight climate change. We need new sources of clean energy, better batteries and more efficient ways of producing food, and all of this depends on research.</p>
  <p>Moreover, investing in science creates qualified jobs and prevents young researchers from leaving the country to find better opportunities abroad.</p>
  <p>Some people argue that there are more urgent needs. Nevertheless, I believe research is an investment in our future, not an expense.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Muestra su acuerdo desde la primera frase, lo justifica con tres argumentos y ejemplos reales (las vacunas, el cambio climático, la fuga de cerebros) y responde a la objeción contraria. Expresiones útiles: <em>the key to</em> + -ing, <em>in record time</em>, <em>prevent someone from</em> + -ing, <em>some people argue that</em>, <em>nevertheless</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Today, personal care and beauty seem more important than ever. In my opinion, taking care of ourselves is positive, but the obsession with beauty can be harmful.</p>
  <p>On the positive side, looking after our appearance is a way of looking after our health. Washing, eating well, doing exercise and dressing in clothes we like can make us feel more confident.</p>
  <p>On the negative side, social media and advertising show perfect bodies and faces, many of them edited. As a result, many young people feel insecure about their appearance and spend a lot of money on cosmetics, treatments or even surgery to look like them.</p>
  <p>Furthermore, people are sometimes judged by their looks rather than by their personality or abilities.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, personal care is important, but we should do it to feel good, not to meet impossible beauty standards.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Distingue entre cuidarse (positivo) y la obsesión por la belleza (negativo), lo que da una postura clara y matizada. Cada idea va en su párrafo. Expresiones útiles: <em>more important than ever</em>, <em>look after</em>, <em>feel insecure about</em>, <em>be judged by</em>, <em>rather than</em>, <em>meet beauty standards</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
