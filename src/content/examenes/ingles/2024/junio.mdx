<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>What do you think about using mobile phones in schools? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>“Young people do not read books anymore.” Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>The use of mobile phones in schools is a controversial issue. In my opinion, they should not be allowed during lessons, although they can be useful in some situations.</p>
  <p>The main reason is that phones are a constant distraction. If students have their phones on the desk, it is very tempting to check notifications or social media instead of paying attention to the teacher. Moreover, banning phones during breaks encourages students to talk and play with each other.</p>
  <p>However, mobile phones can also be a learning tool. Teachers sometimes ask us to look up information or to use educational apps, and in those cases phones are very practical.</p>
  <p>To sum up, I believe phones should stay in our bags most of the time, and only be used when the teacher asks for them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Da una opinión matizada en la introducción y la justifica con argumentos a favor y en contra, como pide <em>Give reasons</em>. Expresiones útiles: <em>a controversial issue</em>, <em>it is very tempting to</em>, <em>instead of</em> + -ing, <em>look up information</em> y la pasiva con modales (<em>should not be allowed</em>, <em>only be used</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Many adults complain that young people do not read books anymore. In my opinion, this statement is partly true, but it is not the whole story.</p>
  <p>It is true that teenagers spend many hours on their phones, watching videos or playing games, and that leaves less time for reading. Besides, reading a long novel requires concentration, which is difficult when we are used to short content.</p>
  <p>However, young people still read, just in a different way. We read articles and messages online, and many teenagers follow book recommendations on social media. In fact, some sagas have become very popular thanks to TikTok, and bookshops have special sections for them.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, young people read less than before, but they have not stopped reading. Schools and families should help them discover books they really enjoy.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Ante una afirmación entre comillas, dice hasta qué punto está de acuerdo (<em>partly true</em>), da la razón en lo que la tiene y luego la matiza con ejemplos. Expresiones útiles: <em>it is not the whole story</em>, <em>be used to</em> + nombre, <em>in fact</em>, <em>thanks to</em>, <em>have not stopped</em> + -ing.</p>
</Redaccion>
