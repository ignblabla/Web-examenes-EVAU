<p class="en-title" lang="es">El examen consta de 3 Bloques (A, B y C)</p>

<p lang="es">En cada bloque (<strong>Comprehension, Use of English</strong> y <strong>Writing</strong>) se plantean varias preguntas, de las que se deberá responder al número que se indica en cada uno. En caso de responder más cuestiones de las requeridas, serán tenidas en cuenta las respondidas en primer lugar hasta alcanzar dicho número. Las preguntas han de ser respondidas en su totalidad: si la pregunta tiene dos secciones, hay que responder ambas.</p>

<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Should we preserve our traditions and/or be open to new ones? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What would you like to do if you won the lottery? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Traditions are an important part of our identity, but societies are always changing. In my opinion, we should preserve our traditions and, at the same time, be open to new ones.</p>
  <p>On the one hand, traditions connect us with our past and our families. Celebrations such as Holy Week or local festivals bring people together and keep our history alive. If we forget them, we lose part of who we are.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, being open to new traditions makes us richer. Thanks to globalisation and immigration, we can enjoy Chinese New Year, Halloween or food from other countries. This helps us understand other cultures and be more tolerant.</p>
  <p>However, some old traditions, especially those that involve cruelty to animals, should change.</p>
  <p>To sum up, the best option is to keep the traditions that make sense today and welcome new ones with an open mind.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La pregunta ofrece dos opciones (<em>and/or</em>) y la redacción elige combinar ambas, justificando cada una en su párrafo. Añade un matiz (<em>However…</em>) que muestra pensamiento crítico. Expresiones útiles: <em>at the same time</em>, <em>bring people together</em>, <em>keep… alive</em>, <em>make sense</em>, <em>with an open mind</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Everyone has dreamt about winning the lottery at some point. If I won a lot of money, I know exactly what I would do with it.</p>
  <p>First of all, I would help my family. I would pay off my parents’ mortgage so that they could stop worrying about money, and I would buy my grandparents a house near the sea, which they have always wanted.</p>
  <p>Secondly, I would invest part of the money in my education. I would like to study abroad, at a good university, and learn several languages.</p>
  <p>Then, of course, I would travel around the world with my best friends, visiting places like Japan, Australia and Canada.</p>
  <p>Finally, I would give some money to charities that help children and protect animals.</p>
  <p>In short, I would not spend it all on myself, because sharing it would make me happier.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Al ser una situación hipotética, todo el texto usa el segundo condicional (<em>If I won…, I would…</em>). Los planes van ordenados con <em>first of all</em>, <em>secondly</em>, <em>then</em>, <em>finally</em>. Expresiones útiles: <em>at some point</em>, <em>pay off a mortgage</em>, <em>so that they could</em>, <em>spend it all on</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
