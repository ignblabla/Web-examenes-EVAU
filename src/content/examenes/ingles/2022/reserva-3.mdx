<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>“Dogs are a man’s best friend,” people say. Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Do you think the role of women in society has changed over the last fifty years? Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>People often say that dogs are a man’s best friend, and I think there are good reasons for this saying.</p>
  <p>First of all, dogs are incredibly loyal. They are always happy to see you, they do not judge you and they stay by your side when you are sad. My dog, Luna, waits for me at the door every day when I come back from school.</p>
  <p>Secondly, dogs help people in many ways. Some are trained to guide blind people, to find people after earthquakes or to work with the police. Others visit hospitals and old people’s homes to keep patients company.</p>
  <p>In addition, having a dog encourages us to go for walks and be more active.</p>
  <p>However, having a dog is a big responsibility. Every year, many dogs are abandoned because their owners get tired of them.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, dogs are wonderful friends, but we must also be good friends to them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Comenta el dicho con argumentos a favor (lealtad, ayuda, actividad) y ejemplos, incluido uno personal, y añade una advertencia sobre el abandono. La conclusión le da la vuelta a la frase del enunciado. Expresiones útiles: <em>stay by your side</em>, <em>be trained to</em>, <em>keep someone company</em>, <em>get tired of</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>I believe the role of women in society has changed enormously over the last fifty years, although there is still work to do.</p>
  <p>Fifty years ago, in Spain, most women stayed at home and looked after their families. Many of them could not study at university or have a career, and they needed their husband’s permission for things like opening a bank account.</p>
  <p>Today, the situation is very different. Women can vote, study and work in any profession, and in fact there are now more women than men at Spanish universities. There are female scientists, politicians and business leaders.</p>
  <p>However, inequality has not disappeared. Women still earn less than men on average, they do most of the housework and they are underrepresented in top positions.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, there has been great progress, but we must continue working for real equality.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Compara el pasado y el presente en dos párrafos (en pasado y en presente) y dedica otro a lo que falta por conseguir. Expresiones útiles: <em>over the last fifty years</em>, <em>there is still work to do</em>, <em>on average</em>, <em>be underrepresented</em>, el present perfect (<em>has changed</em>, <em>has not disappeared</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>
