<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Is it important for you to learn how to cook? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What is your dream job? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Yes, learning how to cook is very important for me, and I think everyone should know the basics.</p>
  <p>The first reason is independence. In a couple of years I will probably go to university in another city, and I do not want to live on pizza and instant noodles. Knowing how to cook will allow me to look after myself.</p>
  <p>Secondly, cooking is good for our health. When you prepare your own meals, you know exactly what you are eating, and you can choose fresh ingredients and avoid processed food. It is also much cheaper than eating out.</p>
  <p>Finally, I find cooking relaxing and fun. On Sundays I often cook with my grandmother, who is teaching me her traditional recipes, and it is one of my favourite moments of the week.</p>
  <p>In short, cooking is a skill for life.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde a la pregunta en la primera línea y da tres razones (independencia, salud, disfrute), con ejemplos personales. Expresiones útiles: <em>know the basics</em>, <em>live on</em> (alimentarse a base de), <em>allow me to</em>, <em>look after myself</em>, <em>eat out</em>, <em>a skill for life</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>My dream job is to be a marine biologist.</p>
  <p>I have loved the sea since I was a child. Every summer, my family and I spend our holidays on the coast, and I have always been fascinated by the animals that live under the water. When I was twelve, I went snorkelling for the first time, and seeing so many colourful fish made me decide what I wanted to do in the future.</p>
  <p>As a marine biologist, I would study marine species and work to protect the oceans, which are in danger because of pollution, plastic and climate change. I would like to work on a research ship or in a marine reserve.</p>
  <p>I know it is a difficult career and that there are not many jobs, but I am willing to work hard.</p>
  <p>For me, a dream job is one that combines passion and purpose.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Nombra la profesión, explica de dónde viene su interés con una pequeña anécdota (en pasado) y describe qué haría (con <em>would</em>). Reconoce las dificultades y termina con una frase redonda. Expresiones útiles: <em>be fascinated by</em>, <em>made me decide</em>, <em>be in danger</em>, <em>be willing to</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
