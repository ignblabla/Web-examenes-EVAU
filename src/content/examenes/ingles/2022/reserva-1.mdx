<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Eating out or eating at home. Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Is your smartphone spying on you? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Eating out and eating at home are both part of our lives, and each option has its advantages.</p>
  <p>Eating out is a great way to socialise and celebrate. Going to a restaurant with friends or family is relaxing, because nobody has to cook or wash the dishes. It is also an opportunity to try new dishes and food from other countries.</p>
  <p>However, eating at home has important benefits. It is much cheaper, and it is usually healthier, because we can choose the ingredients and control the amount of salt, sugar and fat. Besides, cooking together and sharing a meal at the table can bring families closer.</p>
  <p>Personally, I prefer eating at home on a daily basis and keeping restaurants for special occasions. In that way, eating out remains something special.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, both options are good if we find the right balance.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Compara las dos opciones, una en cada párrafo, y da una preferencia personal razonada. Expresiones útiles: <em>a great way to</em>, <em>wash the dishes</em>, <em>bring families closer</em>, <em>on a daily basis</em>, <em>in that way</em>, <em>find the right balance</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Many people have had the strange experience of talking about a product and then seeing an advert for it on their phone. Is our smartphone spying on us? In a way, I think it is.</p>
  <p>Our phones collect a lot of information about us. Apps know where we are thanks to GPS, what we search for, which websites we visit and who we talk to. Companies use this data to show us personalised advertising, and sometimes they sell it to other companies.</p>
  <p>It is not clear whether phones listen to our conversations, but they do not need to: the information we give them voluntarily is enough to predict our tastes.</p>
  <p>To protect our privacy, we should read app permissions carefully, turn off location when we do not need it and avoid accepting all cookies.</p>
  <p>In short, smartphones are not exactly spies, but we should be careful about the information we share.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Empieza con una experiencia que todos conocen, responde con una postura matizada (<em>In a way, I think it is</em>), explica cómo se recogen los datos y propone medidas concretas. Expresiones útiles: <em>search for</em>, <em>it is not clear whether</em>, <em>they do not need to</em>, <em>turn off</em>, <em>be careful about</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
