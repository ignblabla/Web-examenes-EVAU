<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>How could you help people around you? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Do you think that our grandparents used to eat better than we do now? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>There are many ways in which I could help the people around me, and most of them are simple.</p>
  <p>At home, I could help my parents more with the housework, for example by cooking dinner once a week or doing the shopping. That would give them more time to rest after work.</p>
  <p>At school, I could help classmates who have difficulties with subjects I am good at, such as English. Explaining something to others is also a great way of learning it better.</p>
  <p>In my neighbourhood, there are several elderly people who live alone. I could visit them, go to the chemist’s for them or teach them how to use their phones to make video calls with their families.</p>
  <p>Finally, I could join a local charity at weekends.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, helping others does not require much, just a little time and willingness.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Organiza las ideas por lugares (en casa, en el instituto, en el barrio) y usa <em>could</em> para proponer acciones posibles, como pide la pregunta. Expresiones útiles: <em>by</em> + -ing para explicar cómo (<em>by cooking dinner</em>), <em>a great way of</em> + -ing, <em>go to the chemist’s</em>, <em>join a charity</em>, <em>willingness</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Yes, I think our grandparents used to eat better than we do now, although they had less choice.</p>
  <p>Firstly, they used to eat fresh, local food. My grandmother always tells me that they bought fruit, vegetables and fish at the market every day, and that most meals were cooked at home. Today, many people eat processed food and fast food, which contain too much sugar, salt and fat.</p>
  <p>Secondly, they used to follow the Mediterranean diet, with lots of vegetables, pulses and olive oil, and they ate meat only occasionally.</p>
  <p>Moreover, families used to sit down together to eat, without phones or television, and meals were slower.</p>
  <p>However, not everything was better in the past: some families could not afford enough food.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, we should learn from our grandparents and go back to simpler, healthier eating habits.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La pregunta usa <em>used to</em>, y la respuesta lo aprovecha para describir hábitos del pasado y compararlos con los de hoy. Incluye una concesión (<em>However, not everything was better…</em>). Vocabulario útil: <em>processed food</em>, <em>pulses</em> (legumbres), <em>the Mediterranean diet</em>, <em>could not afford</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
