<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Which band, singer or song has most influenced your life? Why?</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What makes you be in a good mood? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Many songs have been important in my life, but the band that has influenced me most is Queen.</p>
  <p>I discovered them when I was ten, thanks to my father, who used to play their albums in the car on long journeys. At first I only liked “We Will Rock You”, but soon I knew all their songs by heart.</p>
  <p>Queen influenced me in several ways. Firstly, they made me want to learn music, so I started piano lessons and now I can play some of their songs. Secondly, Freddie Mercury taught me that it is important to be yourself, even if you are different.</p>
  <p>Moreover, their music has been the soundtrack of many happy moments with my family.</p>
  <p>Even today, when I feel down, listening to “Don’t Stop Me Now” always cheers me up.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Elige un grupo concreto, cuenta cómo lo descubrió y explica en qué le ha influido, respondiendo al <em>Why?</em> del enunciado. Expresiones útiles: <em>used to play</em>, <em>at first</em>, <em>know by heart</em>, <em>made me want to</em>, <em>be yourself</em>, <em>feel down</em>, <em>cheer someone up</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Everyone has bad days, but there are a few things that always put me in a good mood.</p>
  <p>The first one is spending time with my friends. When we get together, we laugh so much that I forget about any problem. Even a simple afternoon in the park with them can change my day.</p>
  <p>Another thing that cheers me up is doing sport. After playing basketball or going for a run, I always feel full of energy and much happier. I have read that exercise releases chemicals in the brain that improve our mood, and I can confirm it.</p>
  <p>Music also helps me a lot. I have a special playlist with my favourite songs for when I feel sad.</p>
  <p>Finally, small things like a sunny day, a good meal or a message from someone I love can make me smile.</p>
  <p>In short, happiness is often in simple things.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Enumera varias cosas que le ponen de buen humor, con ejemplos concretos, y termina con una reflexión general. Expresiones útiles: <em>put me in a good mood</em>, <em>get together</em>, <em>so much that</em>, <em>cheer me up</em>, <em>full of energy</em>, <em>make me smile</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
