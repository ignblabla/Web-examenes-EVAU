<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Music has magical powers. Do you agree? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Do you think it is important to go outdoors and spend time in nature? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Of course, music cannot do real magic, but I agree that it has a special power over us.</p>
  <p>Firstly, music can change our mood in seconds. A cheerful song can make us want to dance on a bad day, and a sad one can help us release our emotions. That is why we choose different music depending on how we feel.</p>
  <p>Secondly, music brings back memories. When I hear certain songs, I immediately remember a summer holiday, a concert with my friends or my grandfather singing in the kitchen.</p>
  <p>Moreover, music unites people. At concerts, thousands of strangers sing the same words together, and many traditions and celebrations would not be the same without music.</p>
  <p>Finally, it is used in hospitals as therapy, which shows its effect on our minds.</p>
  <p>For all these reasons, I believe music really does have something magical.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Muestra su acuerdo con un matiz inicial (<em>Of course, music cannot do real magic, but…</em>) y da cuatro “poderes” de la música, con ejemplos personales. Expresiones útiles: <em>release emotions</em>, <em>depending on</em>, <em>bring back memories</em>, <em>would not be the same without</em> y el <em>does</em> enfático de la conclusión.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Yes, I think going outdoors and spending time in nature is very important, especially nowadays.</p>
  <p>The first reason is our physical health. When we go for a walk in the countryside, cycle or swim in the sea, we exercise without even realising it, and we breathe fresh air instead of the polluted air of the city.</p>
  <p>The second reason is mental health. We spend too many hours in front of screens, and being in nature helps us relax and disconnect. Personally, after a day in the mountains I feel much calmer and sleep better.</p>
  <p>Finally, spending time in nature teaches us to value and respect it. If children never visit forests or beaches, it will be difficult for them to understand why we need to protect them.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, we should all try to spend more time outdoors.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Da tres razones bien diferenciadas (salud física, salud mental, respeto por la naturaleza) y añade su experiencia personal. Expresiones útiles: <em>go for a walk</em>, <em>without even realising it</em>, <em>instead of</em>, <em>disconnect</em>, <em>teach us to value</em> y el primer condicional (<em>If children never visit…, it will be difficult…</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>
