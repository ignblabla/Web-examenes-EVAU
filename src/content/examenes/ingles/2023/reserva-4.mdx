<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Online shopping or traditional shopping? Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What is your ideal school like? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>More and more people buy online, but traditional shops are still popular. Both options have advantages.</p>
  <p>Online shopping is very convenient. You can buy anything at any time without leaving home, compare prices easily and read other customers’ opinions. For people who live in small villages or who do not have much free time, it is a great solution.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, traditional shopping has benefits that the Internet cannot offer. You can see and try on products before buying them, ask the shop assistant for advice and take things home immediately. Moreover, buying in local shops helps small businesses and keeps our town centres alive.</p>
  <p>Personally, I prefer traditional shopping for clothes and shoes, because I like trying them on, but I buy books and electronics online.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, the best option depends on what you are buying.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Compara las dos opciones, una por párrafo, y da una opinión personal razonada que combina ambas según el producto. Expresiones útiles: <em>convenient</em>, <em>without leaving home</em>, <em>try on</em>, <em>ask someone for advice</em>, <em>keep… alive</em>, <em>it depends on</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>My ideal school would be a place where students learn because they want to, not just to pass exams.</p>
  <p>First of all, classes would be more practical. Instead of memorising information, we would do projects, experiments and debates, and we would go on more trips to museums, companies and natural areas.</p>
  <p>Secondly, there would be fewer students in each class, so teachers could pay more attention to each of us and help those who have difficulties.</p>
  <p>As for the building, it would have big windows, green spaces, a modern library and good sports facilities. The timetable would also be more flexible, with time for art, music and sports.</p>
  <p>Finally, students would participate in important decisions, which would make us feel more responsible.</p>
  <p>In short, my ideal school would combine knowledge, creativity and wellbeing.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Describe una escuela imaginaria con el condicional (<em>would be</em>, <em>would do</em>, <em>could pay</em>) y organiza la descripción en aspectos: las clases, el número de alumnos, el edificio y la participación. Expresiones útiles: <em>instead of</em> + -ing, <em>go on trips</em>, <em>pay attention to</em>, <em>as for</em>, <em>wellbeing</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
