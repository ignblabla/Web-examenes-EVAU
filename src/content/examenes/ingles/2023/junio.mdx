<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Helping others is important in today’s world. Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>If you travelled to a new city on holiday, what would you like to do? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>We live in a fast and competitive world where people often think only about themselves. That is precisely why I believe helping others is more important than ever.</p>
  <p>Firstly, helping others makes society fairer. Many people are going through difficult situations, such as unemployment, illness or loneliness, and a little help can make a big difference to their lives.</p>
  <p>Secondly, helping others is also good for us. Volunteers often say that they receive more than they give, because helping makes them feel useful and happier.</p>
  <p>Besides, we do not need to do great things. Small gestures, like helping a classmate with homework, carrying an elderly neighbour’s shopping or donating clothes, also count.</p>
  <p>To sum up, if everyone helped a little, the world would be a much better place.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Muestra su acuerdo con la afirmación y lo justifica con dos argumentos (para la sociedad y para uno mismo) y ejemplos de gestos sencillos. Expresiones útiles: <em>that is precisely why</em>, <em>go through</em>, <em>make a big difference to</em>, <em>also count</em> y el segundo condicional final (<em>if everyone helped…, the world would be…</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>If I travelled to a new city on holiday, I would try to discover it like a local, not just like a tourist.</p>
  <p>First of all, I would explore the city on foot. Walking is the best way to discover hidden corners, small squares and local shops that do not appear in guidebooks. I would also visit the most famous monuments and at least one museum, because I love history.</p>
  <p>Secondly, I would try the local food. I think food says a lot about a culture, so I would avoid international chains and eat in traditional restaurants and markets.</p>
  <p>Finally, I would try to talk to local people. They can recommend the best places and tell you things about the city that you would never learn otherwise.</p>
  <p>In my opinion, this is the best way to really get to know a place.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La pregunta está en condicional y la respuesta lo mantiene todo el texto (<em>If I travelled…, I would…</em>). Da tres planes con su razón (<em>because…</em>, <em>so…</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>on foot</em>, <em>hidden corners</em>, <em>say a lot about</em>, <em>otherwise</em>, <em>get to know a place</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
