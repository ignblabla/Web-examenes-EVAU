<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>How do you see yourself in twenty years? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>“Dressed to impress.” Discuss the role of clothes in today’s society.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>It is difficult to know what will happen in twenty years, but I have a clear idea of how I would like my life to be.</p>
  <p>Professionally, I see myself working as a doctor. I will have finished my degree and my specialisation, and I hope to be working in a hospital, probably in paediatrics, because I love children. I would also like to take part in international projects to help people in poorer countries.</p>
  <p>As for my personal life, I imagine myself living in a house near the sea with my partner and maybe a couple of children. I would also like to have a dog and enough free time to travel and play sport.</p>
  <p>Above all, I hope to be healthy, to still be in touch with my current friends and to feel that I am doing something useful.</p>
  <p>Only time will tell if my dreams come true.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Separa la vida profesional y la personal, y usa una buena variedad de tiempos de futuro: el futuro perfecto (<em>I will have finished</em>), el futuro continuo (<em>I hope to be working</em>) y <em>would like to</em>. Expresiones útiles: <em>as for</em>, <em>above all</em>, <em>be in touch with</em>, <em>only time will tell</em>, <em>come true</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>The expression “dressed to impress” shows that clothes are much more than something to keep us warm. In today’s society, they play an important role.</p>
  <p>Firstly, clothes are a way of expressing our personality. Through our style we show our tastes, the music we like or the group we belong to, especially when we are teenagers.</p>
  <p>Secondly, clothes influence how other people see us. Although we should not judge others by their appearance, first impressions matter. In a job interview, for example, dressing appropriately can make you look more professional and reliable.</p>
  <p>However, the fashion industry pushes us to buy new clothes all the time, which is expensive and very harmful to the environment.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, clothes are important because they communicate who we are, but we should choose them to feel comfortable and confident, not just to impress others.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Parte de la expresión del enunciado, explica dos funciones de la ropa (expresión personal e impresión en los demás) y añade una crítica a la moda rápida. Expresiones útiles: <em>much more than</em>, <em>play an important role</em>, <em>belong to</em>, <em>first impressions matter</em>, <em>be harmful to</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
