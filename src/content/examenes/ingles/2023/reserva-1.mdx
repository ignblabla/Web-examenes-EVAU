<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>What movies, books or TV shows had the biggest impact on your childhood? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Advantages and disadvantages of working from home. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Many films, books and TV shows were part of my childhood, but the ones that had the biggest impact on me were the Harry Potter books.</p>
  <p>My father gave me the first book when I was eight, and I could not stop reading. I finished the seven books in less than a year, and I remember waiting impatiently for my birthday to get the next one.</p>
  <p>These books changed me in two ways. First, they made me love reading. Before Harry Potter, I thought books were boring, but after that I started reading all kinds of stories.</p>
  <p>Second, they taught me important values, such as friendship, courage and standing up for what is right, even when it is difficult.</p>
  <p>I also loved the films, which I watched many times with my sister. Even today, whenever I see them, they remind me of my childhood.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Elige una obra concreta, cuenta brevemente cómo la descubrió (en pasado) y explica dos formas en que le influyó, que es lo que pide la pregunta. Expresiones útiles: <em>could not stop</em> + -ing, <em>impatiently</em>, <em>all kinds of</em>, <em>stand up for what is right</em>, <em>remind me of</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Since the pandemic, working from home has become very common. Like everything, it has advantages and disadvantages.</p>
  <p>On the positive side, remote workers save a lot of time and money because they do not have to commute every day. They can also organise their timetable more freely, which makes it easier to combine work and family life. In addition, fewer cars on the roads means less pollution.</p>
  <p>On the negative side, working from home can be lonely. Many people miss their colleagues and the social contact of the office. Moreover, it is sometimes difficult to separate work from personal life, and some people end up working longer hours.</p>
  <p>In my opinion, the best solution is a combination: working some days at home and others in the office. In this way, workers can enjoy the benefits of both options.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Estructura clásica de ventajas e inconvenientes, con tres ideas en cada párrafo, y una opinión propia en la conclusión (el modelo mixto). Expresiones útiles: <em>save time and money</em>, <em>commute</em>, <em>which makes it easier to</em>, <em>miss</em> (echar de menos), <em>end up</em> + -ing, <em>in this way</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
