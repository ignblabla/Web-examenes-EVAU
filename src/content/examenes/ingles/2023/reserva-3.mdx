<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>Do you like rituals and ceremonies? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>What are your favourite apps? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>Rituals and ceremonies are present in every culture, from weddings and graduations to religious celebrations. Personally, I like them, although not all of them.</p>
  <p>What I like most about ceremonies is that they mark important moments in our lives. Last year, for example, my cousin got married, and the ceremony was very emotional. The whole family was there, and it was a day we will always remember.</p>
  <p>I also enjoy small family rituals, such as eating together every Sunday at my grandparents’ house or eating twelve grapes on New Year’s Eve. These habits make me feel part of something.</p>
  <p>However, I find some ceremonies too long and formal. At some events you have to wear uncomfortable clothes and listen to endless speeches.</p>
  <p>In short, I like rituals when they bring people together and have a real meaning for me.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde con un sí matizado y lo explica con ejemplos propios de grandes ceremonias y de pequeños rituales familiares, y luego con lo que no le gusta. Expresiones útiles: <em>what I like most about… is that</em>, <em>mark important moments</em>, <em>make me feel part of</em>, <em>endless speeches</em>, <em>in short</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Like most teenagers, I have lots of apps on my phone, but there are three that I use every day.</p>
  <p>The first one is WhatsApp. I use it to talk to my friends, organise plans and keep in touch with my family. My class also has a group where we share homework and exam dates, which is really useful.</p>
  <p>The second one is Spotify. I listen to music all the time: on the way to school, while I study and when I go running. I love creating playlists for every mood.</p>
  <p>Finally, I use a language app to practise English and French for about fifteen minutes a day. It is like a game, so learning becomes fun.</p>
  <p>Although I enjoy these apps, I try not to spend too much time on my phone, especially before going to bed.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Presenta tres aplicaciones con un orden claro (<em>the first one</em>, <em>the second one</em>, <em>finally</em>) y explica para qué usa cada una con detalles concretos. Termina con una reflexión personal. Expresiones útiles: <em>keep in touch with</em>, <em>on the way to</em>, <em>for every mood</em>, <em>try not to</em>, <em>before going to bed</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
