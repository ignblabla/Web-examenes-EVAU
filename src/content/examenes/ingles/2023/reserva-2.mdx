<p class="en-option"><strong>III * WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>29</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p>29.1. <strong>How important is physical appearance today? Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p>29.2. <strong>Some music styles are better than others. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.1" idioma="en">
  <p>In theory, what matters is what a person is like inside. However, in practice, physical appearance seems to be very important in today’s society.</p>
  <p>One reason for this is social media. We see thousands of images of people with perfect bodies every day, and many users edit their photos to look better. As a result, many teenagers feel pressure to look a certain way.</p>
  <p>Another reason is that appearance influences first impressions. Some studies suggest that attractive people find it easier to get a job or to be trusted, which is clearly unfair.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, more and more campaigns now show different types of bodies, and many young people are learning to accept themselves as they are.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, appearance is still too important today. We should value people for their personality and abilities, not for their looks.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Contrapone teoría y práctica en la introducción, explica dos causas de la importancia de la apariencia y menciona una tendencia positiva antes de concluir con su opinión. Expresiones útiles: <em>in theory… in practice</em>, <em>feel pressure to</em>, <em>look a certain way</em>, <em>find it easier to</em>, <em>accept themselves as they are</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · 29.2" idioma="en">
  <p>Many people think their favourite type of music is better than the rest. In my opinion, no music style is better than another; it is all a question of taste.</p>
  <p>First of all, music is about emotions. A song that makes one person cry can leave another person indifferent. What matters is how it makes you feel, not whether it is classical, rock or reggaeton.</p>
  <p>Secondly, every style has good and bad songs. There are brilliant pop songs and boring classical pieces, and vice versa. It is more useful to talk about the quality of each song than about genres.</p>
  <p>It is true that some styles are more complex musically, such as jazz or classical music. However, complexity does not necessarily make music better.</p>
  <p>To sum up, instead of criticising other people’s tastes, we should be open to discovering new styles.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Rebate la afirmación del enunciado desde el principio y la argumenta con dos razones y una concesión (<em>It is true that…, However…</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>a question of taste</em>, <em>leave someone indifferent</em>, <em>whether… or…</em>, <em>vice versa</em>, <em>not necessarily</em>, <em>be open to</em> + -ing.</p>
</Redaccion>
