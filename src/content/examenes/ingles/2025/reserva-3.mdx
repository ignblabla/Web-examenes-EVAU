<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Describe a trip you would consider perfect: Where would you go, and what would you do?</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. How does social media influence the preferences of people? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>If I could plan the perfect trip, I would travel around Japan for three weeks with my best friends.</p>
  <p>First, we would spend a few days in Tokyo. I would love to visit its modern neighbourhoods, try real sushi and ramen, and go to the famous crossing in Shibuya. As I am a big fan of manga, I would also visit shops and museums about it.</p>
  <p>Then, we would take the bullet train to Kyoto, which is full of ancient temples and gardens. I would like to stay in a traditional house, sleep on a tatami and wear a kimono for a day.</p>
  <p>Finally, we would spend some time in the countryside, near Mount Fuji, to relax in hot springs and enjoy nature.</p>
  <p>For me, this trip would be perfect because it combines modern cities, history, nature and delicious food.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde a las dos preguntas (dónde iría y qué haría) y organiza el viaje en etapas con <em>first</em>, <em>then</em>, <em>finally</em>. Como es una situación imaginaria, usa el segundo condicional (<em>If I could…, I would…</em>) y <em>would love to</em> / <em>would like to</em> durante todo el texto.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Social media has a huge influence on what people like, buy and even think, often without them realising it.</p>
  <p>Firstly, it affects what we buy. Influencers show products in their posts and videos, and their followers want to have the same clothes, make-up or gadgets. Many brands pay influencers because they know this kind of advertising is more effective than traditional adverts.</p>
  <p>Secondly, algorithms decide what we see. If we watch one video about a topic, the app shows us many more, so our tastes in music, food or fashion become more and more similar to what is popular online. This is how some songs or restaurants suddenly become trends.</p>
  <p>Finally, social media can also influence our opinions, because we usually follow people who think like us.</p>
  <p>In short, social media shapes our preferences in many ways. That is why we should be aware of it and think for ourselves.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> <em>Explain</em> pide explicar cómo ocurre algo, y la redacción da tres mecanismos concretos (los influencers, los algoritmos, las opiniones), cada uno con un ejemplo. Expresiones útiles: <em>without them realising it</em>, <em>this kind of</em>, <em>more and more</em>, <em>this is how</em>, <em>shape</em>, <em>be aware of</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
