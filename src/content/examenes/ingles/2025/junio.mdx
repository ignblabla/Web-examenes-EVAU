<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using apps to meet other people?</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. The importance of listening to your parents. Should we always listen?</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>Dating and friendship apps have become very common, especially among young adults. Like most things, they have both advantages and disadvantages.</p>
  <p>On the positive side, these apps make it easy to meet people you would never meet otherwise. They are very useful for those who have just moved to a new city or who are shy. Besides, you can find people with similar interests, which makes conversation easier.</p>
  <p>On the negative side, you can never be completely sure who you are talking to. Some users lie about their age or appearance, and meeting a stranger can be dangerous. Moreover, these apps can make relationships feel superficial, because people are judged by a photo in a few seconds.</p>
  <p>In my opinion, apps can be a good way to meet people, as long as we use them carefully: meeting in public places and telling a friend where we are going.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La pregunta pide ventajas e inconvenientes, y la redacción dedica un párrafo a cada uno (<em>On the positive side… On the negative side…</em>) antes de dar su opinión con una condición (<em>as long as</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>otherwise</em>, <em>those who</em>, <em>which makes… easier</em>, <em>you can never be completely sure</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Listening to our parents is important, but I do not think we should always do exactly what they say.</p>
  <p>On the one hand, parents usually have more experience than we do. They have already made many of the mistakes we are about to make, so their advice can save us a lot of trouble. Furthermore, most parents only want the best for their children, even when their rules seem unfair.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, parents are not always right. Sometimes they see the world in a different way because they grew up in another time. When we have to make important decisions, such as what to study, we should listen to their opinion, but the final decision should be ours.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, we should always listen to our parents and respect their opinions, but listening does not mean always obeying. The key is to talk and reach an agreement.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde directamente a <em>Should we always listen?</em> con una postura matizada y la desarrolla con argumentos a favor y en contra. La conclusión distingue entre <em>listening</em> y <em>obeying</em>, una idea propia que da calidad al texto. Expresiones útiles: <em>be about to</em>, <em>save us a lot of trouble</em>, <em>reach an agreement</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
