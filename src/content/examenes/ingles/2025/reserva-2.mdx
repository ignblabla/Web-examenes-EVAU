<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Share a memorable story from your childhood.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Do you think everyone has the same opportunities for education and self-improvement? Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>One of my best childhood memories is the day I got lost at the beach when I was six years old.</p>
  <p>It was a hot August afternoon and the beach was very crowded. My parents were reading under the umbrella, and I was building a sandcastle near the water. When I finished, I wanted to show it to them, but when I looked around, every umbrella looked the same.</p>
  <p>I started walking and, after a few minutes, I realised I was completely lost. I began to cry, and a lifeguard came to help me. He took me to his tower and announced my name through a loudspeaker.</p>
  <p>Ten minutes later, my mother arrived running, with tears in her eyes. She hugged me so tightly that I could hardly breathe.</p>
  <p>Today we laugh about it, but since then I have always remembered where our umbrella is.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Una historia breve con planteamiento, nudo y desenlace, y un final con humor que conecta con el presente. Usa el pasado continuo para el contexto (<em>were reading</em>, <em>was building</em>) y el pasado simple para las acciones. Expresiones útiles: <em>get lost</em>, <em>look around</em>, <em>realise</em>, <em>so tightly that</em>, <em>since then</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>In theory, everyone in our society has the right to education. However, I do not think everyone really has the same opportunities to study and improve themselves.</p>
  <p>Firstly, money still makes a big difference. Families with a good income can pay for private lessons, language courses or a year abroad, while others cannot even afford a computer or a quiet place to study at home.</p>
  <p>Secondly, where you live matters. Students in small villages often have to travel a long way to go to secondary school or university, and they have fewer cultural activities around them.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, things have improved a lot. Public education is free, there are scholarships, and the Internet offers free courses to anyone with a connection.</p>
  <p>To conclude, although there is more equality than in the past, we still need more scholarships and resources to make sure that talent, and not money, decides people’s future.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Distingue entre la teoría y la realidad desde la primera frase, da dos argumentos (dinero y lugar de residencia) y reconoce los avances antes de concluir con una propuesta. Expresiones útiles: <em>in theory</em>, <em>make a big difference</em>, <em>cannot even afford</em>, <em>where you live matters</em>, <em>not… but…</em> (<em>talent, and not money</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>
