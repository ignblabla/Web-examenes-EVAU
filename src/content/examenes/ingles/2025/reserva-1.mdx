<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Share a memorable experience you’ve had during your favourite holiday or celebration.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Holidays and festivities encourage us to spend more than we should. Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>My favourite celebration is Holy Week in my town, and the most memorable experience I have had was two years ago, when I took part in a procession for the first time.</p>
  <p>I had watched the processions every year since I was a child, but that year my uncle asked me to join his brotherhood. I was very nervous because I had to walk for six hours, carrying a candle and wearing the traditional robe.</p>
  <p>When we left the church, the streets were full of people and there was complete silence. Suddenly, a woman started singing a saeta from a balcony, and I got goosebumps. At that moment I understood why this tradition means so much to my family.</p>
  <p>I finished exhausted, with sore feet, but very proud. It is an experience I will never forget.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Es una narración: sitúa la historia (cuándo y dónde), cuenta los hechos en orden y termina con lo que sintió. Combina bien los tiempos del pasado: <em>had watched</em> (pluscuamperfecto, antes de la historia), <em>was very nervous</em>, <em>started singing</em>. Expresiones útiles: <em>take part in</em>, <em>suddenly</em>, <em>at that moment</em>, <em>I got goosebumps</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>It is true that holidays and festivities often make us spend more money than we should, and in my opinion this is mainly because of advertising and social pressure.</p>
  <p>Before Christmas, for example, shops and websites are full of offers, and adverts tell us that we need to buy presents to make our loved ones happy. Events such as Black Friday also encourage us to buy things we do not really need.</p>
  <p>Moreover, we often feel we have to keep up with others. If our friends give expensive presents or go out for dinner every night, it is difficult to say no.</p>
  <p>However, celebrations do not have to be expensive. Spending time with family, cooking together or making handmade presents can be even more special.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, I agree with the statement, but we can avoid overspending by making a budget and remembering what really matters.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Ante una afirmación para discutir, dice desde el principio si está de acuerdo y por qué. Da dos causas con ejemplos (Navidad, Black Friday), propone alternativas y vuelve a la afirmación en la conclusión. Expresiones útiles: <em>it is true that</em>, <em>keep up with others</em>, <em>do not have to</em>, <em>what really matters</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
