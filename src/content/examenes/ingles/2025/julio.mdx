<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. How do you balance work and rest in your daily routine?</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Do you think young people sleep enough nowadays? Discuss.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>As a second-year Bachillerato student, finding a balance between work and rest is not always easy, but I try to organise my day carefully.</p>
  <p>On weekdays, I go to school in the morning and, after lunch, I study for about three hours. I make a timetable at the beginning of each week, so I know what I have to do every day. I also take a short break every hour to stretch my legs or have a snack, because I cannot concentrate for too long.</p>
  <p>To rest, I go to the gym three evenings a week, which helps me forget about exams for a while. At weekends, I spend time with my friends and family and try not to study on Saturday afternoons.</p>
  <p>Finally, I make sure I sleep at least eight hours. In my experience, resting well is just as important as studying hard.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Describe una rutina real con detalles concretos (horarios, descansos, deporte) y usa el presente simple para hábitos. Termina con una idea general sacada de su experiencia. Expresiones útiles: <em>find a balance between</em>, <em>take a break</em>, <em>forget about</em>, <em>make sure</em>, <em>just as important as</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Experts recommend that teenagers sleep between eight and ten hours a night. However, I think most young people today do not sleep enough.</p>
  <p>The main reason is the use of mobile phones. Many teenagers stay awake until very late chatting with friends, watching videos or scrolling through social media. In addition, the light from screens makes it more difficult to fall asleep.</p>
  <p>Another reason is stress. Young people have a lot of homework and exams, and some of them also do sports or other activities in the afternoon, so they end up studying late at night.</p>
  <p>The consequences are serious: tired students find it hard to pay attention in class, they are in a worse mood and their health can suffer.</p>
  <p>To sum up, young people need to sleep more. Leaving the phone outside the bedroom and organising study time better would be a good start.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Abre con un dato general, da su opinión y la justifica con dos causas y sus consecuencias. Cierra con soluciones prácticas. Expresiones útiles: <em>stay awake</em>, <em>fall asleep</em>, <em>end up</em> + -ing, <em>find it hard to</em>, <em>would be a good start</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
