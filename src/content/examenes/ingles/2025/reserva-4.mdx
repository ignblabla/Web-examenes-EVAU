<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF AT LEAST 120 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. In your opinion, what is the most revolutionary scientific innovation in history? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Telepathy: What would you use it for?</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>There have been many great scientific innovations, but in my opinion the most revolutionary one is the discovery of antibiotics.</p>
  <p>Before penicillin was discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928, people could die from an infected cut or from diseases such as pneumonia. Doctors had very few ways to fight bacteria, and life expectancy was much lower than today.</p>
  <p>Antibiotics changed everything. They have saved millions of lives, and they made other medical advances possible. For example, complex operations and organ transplants would be extremely dangerous without them, because of the risk of infection.</p>
  <p>Of course, other inventions such as electricity or the Internet have also changed our lives completely. However, I believe nothing is more important than health.</p>
  <p>Nevertheless, we must use antibiotics responsibly, because bacteria are becoming resistant to them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Elige una innovación concreta y la justifica con datos y ejemplos (Fleming, 1928, los trasplantes). Compara con otras opciones antes de reafirmar su elección y añade una advertencia final. Expresiones útiles: <em>the most revolutionary one</em>, la pasiva (<em>was discovered by</em>), <em>made… possible</em>, <em>nothing is more important than</em>, <em>nevertheless</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Being able to read other people’s minds sounds like a superpower from a film. If I had telepathy, I would use it, but very carefully.</p>
  <p>First of all, I would use it to help people. Many people hide their problems because they are ashamed or afraid, so I could know when a friend is sad and needs support, even if they say they are fine.</p>
  <p>I would also use it to communicate with people who cannot speak, such as babies or people with certain illnesses. Understanding what they need would make their lives much easier.</p>
  <p>However, I would never use it to read someone’s private thoughts just out of curiosity, or to cheat in an exam. Everyone has the right to privacy, and knowing everything others think could also hurt me.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, telepathy could be a wonderful gift if it were used to help others, not to control them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Un tema imaginativo se resuelve con el segundo condicional (<em>If I had…, I would…</em>, <em>if it were used</em>): dos usos positivos, un límite ético y una conclusión. Expresiones útiles: <em>be able to</em>, <em>be ashamed</em>, <em>even if</em>, <em>out of curiosity</em>, <em>have the right to</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
