<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. How could your hometown be improved? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Do you believe social media influences how teenagers see themselves? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>I really like my hometown, but there are a few things that could be improved to make life better for the people who live there.</p>
  <p>To begin with, public transport should be better. At the moment, buses only run once an hour and stop very early, so teenagers like me depend on our parents to go anywhere in the evening. More frequent buses would also reduce traffic and pollution in the centre.</p>
  <p>Secondly, there are not enough places for young people. Apart from a couple of cafés, there is nowhere to meet when it rains. A youth centre with a library, a study room and space for activities such as music or theatre would be a great idea.</p>
  <p>Finally, I think the town needs more green areas. Some streets are full of cars, and planting trees and creating a park would make them much more pleasant, especially in summer.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, with better transport, more facilities for young people and more green spaces, my hometown would be an even better place to live.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Propone tres mejoras concretas, cada una en su párrafo y con su justificación. Usa estructuras para proponer cambios, que es lo que pide la pregunta: <em>should be</em>, <em>would be a great idea</em>, <em>needs more</em>, y el condicional (<em>More frequent buses would also reduce…</em>). Conectores de orden: <em>to begin with</em>, <em>secondly</em>, <em>finally</em>, <em>in conclusion</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>In my opinion, social media has a strong influence on how teenagers see themselves, and this influence is often negative.</p>
  <p>The main problem is that social media shows an unrealistic image of life. Influencers post perfect photos, many of them edited with filters, and teenagers compare their own bodies and lives with them. As a result, many young people feel that they are not attractive, popular or successful enough.</p>
  <p>Furthermore, the number of likes and followers can become a measure of self-esteem. When a post does not get as many likes as expected, some teenagers feel rejected, even though it does not really mean anything.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, social media can also have a positive effect. It allows young people to find others with the same interests and to share their talents, which can make them feel more confident.</p>
  <p>To conclude, I believe social media does influence how teenagers see themselves. That is why it is important to use it critically and remember that what we see online is not always real.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Toma postura desde el principio, la defiende con dos argumentos y reconoce la otra cara (<em>On the other hand</em>) antes de concluir. Expresiones útiles: <em>in my opinion</em>, <em>as a result</em>, <em>furthermore</em>, <em>even though</em>, <em>that is why</em>, y el <em>do</em> enfático de la conclusión (<em>social media does influence</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>
