<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Do video games have positive or negative effects on children and teenagers? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. How do you use Artificial Intelligence in your daily life? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>Video games are one of the most popular hobbies among children and teenagers. In my opinion, they can have both positive and negative effects, depending on how they are used.</p>
  <p>On the one hand, video games have some clear benefits. Many of them develop skills such as problem solving, concentration and quick decision-making. Moreover, online games allow young people to cooperate with others and make friends who share their interests. Some games are even used in schools to teach history or science.</p>
  <p>On the other hand, spending too many hours playing can be harmful. Some teenagers become addicted and neglect their studies, sleep less or stop doing physical exercise. In addition, certain games are very violent and are not suitable for children, even though they play them anyway.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, I believe video games are not good or bad in themselves. The problem is the excessive use. Parents should control the time their children spend playing and check that the games are appropriate for their age.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Es la estructura clásica de ventajas e inconvenientes: introducción con la postura, un párrafo para cada lado (<em>On the one hand… On the other hand…</em>) y una conclusión con una propuesta. Expresiones útiles: <em>depending on</em>, <em>skills such as</em>, <em>become addicted</em>, <em>not good or bad in themselves</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Artificial Intelligence has become part of my daily life, often without me even noticing it.</p>
  <p>The most obvious way I use AI is for studying. When I do not understand something in Maths or Physics, I sometimes ask a chatbot to explain it step by step or to give me more examples. However, I always check the answers with my textbook, because AI can make mistakes.</p>
  <p>I also use AI in other, less obvious ways. My phone uses it to recognise my face, to suggest the next word when I type and to organise my photos. Besides, my music and video apps recommend songs and series based on what I have watched before.</p>
  <p>Finally, I sometimes use translation tools when I read texts in other languages, although I try not to depend on them too much.</p>
  <p>In short, AI makes many daily tasks easier for me. Even so, I think it is important to use it as a tool and not let it do our thinking for us.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde a <em>How do you use…?</em> con usos concretos de su vida diaria, ordenados de más a menos evidente, y añade una reflexión personal al final. Expresiones útiles: <em>without me even noticing it</em>, <em>step by step</em>, <em>based on</em>, <em>try not to</em>, <em>in short</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
