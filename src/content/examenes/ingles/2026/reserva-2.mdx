<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Why is speaking foreign languages important today? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Would you like to be on a reality show? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>In today’s globalised world, speaking foreign languages is more important than ever, for several reasons.</p>
  <p>To begin with, languages open the door to better jobs. Many companies work with clients from other countries, so they look for employees who can communicate in English, French or German. A person who speaks several languages has many more opportunities, both in Spain and abroad.</p>
  <p>In addition, languages are essential for studying and travelling. Thanks to programmes such as Erasmus, thousands of students spend a year in another country, and knowing the language makes the experience much richer. When you travel, you can talk to local people instead of just visiting monuments.</p>
  <p>Finally, learning a language means discovering a new culture. You understand films, songs and books in their original version, and you learn to see the world from a different point of view.</p>
  <p>In conclusion, foreign languages are not just another school subject. They are a key that opens doors to work, education and other cultures, so it is worth making the effort to learn them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Tres razones bien diferenciadas (trabajo, estudios y viajes, cultura), cada una con un ejemplo. La conclusión resume con una imagen (<em>a key that opens doors</em>) en lugar de repetir lo dicho. Expresiones útiles: <em>more important than ever</em>, <em>in addition</em>, <em>thanks to</em>, <em>instead of</em> + -ing, <em>it is worth</em> + -ing.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Reality shows are very popular, and some of my friends say they would love to take part in one. However, I would not like to be on a reality show, for several reasons.</p>
  <p>The first reason is privacy. On these programmes, cameras follow you twenty-four hours a day, and millions of people watch everything you do and say. I think that would make me feel very uncomfortable, because everyone needs some private moments.</p>
  <p>Secondly, the image of the contestants is often manipulated. The producers choose the scenes that create more drama, so viewers may get a completely wrong idea of who you really are. Some former contestants have received insults on social media because of this.</p>
  <p>Admittedly, being on a reality show can bring fame and money, and it could be an unforgettable experience. Even so, I do not think it is worth losing your privacy and your reputation.</p>
  <p>All things considered, I prefer to watch reality shows from my sofa rather than be on one.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> La respuesta (<em>I would not like…</em>) aparece en la introducción, con dos razones desarrolladas y una concesión (<em>Admittedly…, Even so…</em>) que demuestra que se han tenido en cuenta otros puntos de vista. Fíjate en el uso de <em>would</em> para situaciones hipotéticas y en <em>prefer to… rather than…</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
