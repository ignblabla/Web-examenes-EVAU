<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. What is your favorite celebration? Give reasons.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Is it important to preserve cultural heritage? Explain.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>Of all the celebrations of the year, my favourite one is Christmas, and there are several reasons for this.</p>
  <p>First of all, it is the only time of the year when my whole family gets together. My cousins live in different cities, so on Christmas Eve we all travel to my grandparents’ house. We have a huge dinner, we laugh a lot and we stay up talking until very late.</p>
  <p>Another reason is the atmosphere. In December the streets are decorated with lights, there are Christmas markets and everybody seems to be in a better mood. I also love decorating the tree with my younger brother, which has become a tradition for us.</p>
  <p>Last but not least, there are no classes for two weeks, so I can rest, read and spend time with my friends.</p>
  <p>Of course, some people think Christmas has become too commercial, and I partly agree. However, for me it is above all about family. That is why it is my favourite celebration.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Nombra la celebración en la primera frase y da tres razones con ejemplos de su experiencia. Antes de la conclusión responde a una posible objeción (<em>some people think…, and I partly agree. However…</em>), un recurso que se valora mucho. Expresiones útiles: <em>get together</em>, <em>stay up</em>, <em>last but not least</em>, <em>above all</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>Cultural heritage includes monuments, traditions, languages and works of art that have been passed down from one generation to the next. In my opinion, preserving it is very important.</p>
  <p>Firstly, cultural heritage tells us who we are. Buildings such as the Alhambra or local festivals show how people lived and what they believed in the past. If we lose them, we lose part of our identity and our history.</p>
  <p>Secondly, heritage has economic benefits. Millions of tourists visit Spain every year to see its monuments and traditions, and this creates jobs in many towns and cities.</p>
  <p>However, preserving heritage is not easy. Old buildings need expensive restoration, and mass tourism can damage them. For this reason, governments should invest in their protection and visitors should respect the rules.</p>
  <p>To sum up, our cultural heritage is a treasure that connects us with the past and helps us in the present. It is our responsibility to look after it so that future generations can also enjoy it.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Empieza definiendo el concepto, algo muy útil cuando el tema es abstracto, y luego da dos argumentos con ejemplos (la Alhambra, el turismo). Incluye los problemas y una propuesta (<em>governments should…</em>). Expresiones útiles: <em>passed down from one generation to the next</em>, <em>firstly</em>, <em>secondly</em>, <em>for this reason</em>, <em>so that</em> para expresar finalidad.</p>
</Redaccion>
