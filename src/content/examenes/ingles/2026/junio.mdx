<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. What is the role of music or art in your life? Explain.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. What kind of TV series do you like best? Give reasons.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>Music and art have always played an important role in my life, although in different ways. Music is part of my daily routine, while art is something I enjoy at special moments.</p>
  <p>First of all, I listen to music almost all the time: on the bus, while I study and when I go running. It helps me concentrate and, when I am stressed because of exams, a good song can change my mood in seconds. Moreover, I have been learning to play the guitar for three years, and playing with my friends is one of the things I enjoy most.</p>
  <p>As for art, I am not very good at drawing, but I love visiting museums. Last summer I went to the Prado with my family, and seeing Velázquez’s paintings in real life was unforgettable.</p>
  <p>To sum up, music keeps me company every day and art makes me see the world differently. I cannot imagine my life without them.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Responde a la pregunta en la introducción y dedica un párrafo a cada parte (música y arte), con ejemplos personales. Expresiones útiles: <em>play an important role</em>, <em>first of all</em>, <em>moreover</em>, <em>as for</em>, <em>to sum up</em>, y el present perfect continuous para algo que empezó en el pasado y sigue (<em>I have been learning… for three years</em>).</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>There are so many TV series nowadays that it is difficult to choose, but the ones I like best are crime and mystery series.</p>
  <p>The main reason is that they keep me guessing until the very end. While I am watching, I try to work out who the criminal is, and I feel very satisfied when I get it right. Besides, good crime series usually have complex characters, so you never know who you can trust.</p>
  <p>Another reason is that they are perfect to watch with other people. My sister and I often watch an episode together after dinner, and then we spend a long time discussing our theories. In this way, a series becomes something we share, not just a way of killing time.</p>
  <p>However, I must admit that some of them are quite violent, so they are not suitable for everyone.</p>
  <p>All in all, I prefer crime series because they are exciting, they make me think and they bring people together.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Da su preferencia en la primera frase y la justifica con dos razones, una por párrafo, como pide <em>Give reasons</em>. Añade un matiz con <em>However</em> antes de concluir, lo que muestra madurez. Expresiones útiles: <em>the main reason is that</em>, <em>besides</em>, <em>another reason is that</em>, <em>I must admit that</em>, <em>all in all</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
