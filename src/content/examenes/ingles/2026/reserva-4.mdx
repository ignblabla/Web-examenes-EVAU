<p class="en-option"><strong>WRITING (3 points)</strong></p>

<p class="en-task"><strong>C</strong>. WRITE A COMPOSITION OF 140–180 WORDS ABOUT ONE OF THE TOPICS PROPOSED AND FOCUS ON IT. CHOOSE <strong><u>ONE TOPIC</u></strong> ONLY:</p>

<div class="en-list en-topics">
  <p><strong>C1. Why should we care about our elderly people? Discuss.</strong></p>
  <p><strong>C2. Is there a person you admire in your family? Explain why.</strong></p>
</div>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C1" idioma="en">
  <p>Our society is getting older, and more and more people are over seventy. I firmly believe that caring about our elderly people is not only a duty but also something that benefits all of us.</p>
  <p>Firstly, we owe them a lot. Our grandparents worked hard, often in difficult conditions, to give their children a better life. Looking after them when they need help is a way of thanking them for everything they did.</p>
  <p>Secondly, older people have a lot to teach us. They have experienced things we can only read about in books, and listening to their stories helps us understand our history and our own family.</p>
  <p>Unfortunately, many elderly people feel lonely, especially those who live on their own in big cities. Simple things such as visiting them, calling them regularly or helping them with technology can make a huge difference.</p>
  <p>To conclude, caring about the elderly is a question of gratitude and respect. Besides, one day we will be old too, and we will want others to care about us.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> <em>Discuss</em> pide argumentar, y aquí hay dos razones (gratitud y lo que aprendemos de ellos), un problema real (la soledad) con soluciones sencillas y una conclusión que hace reflexionar. Expresiones útiles: <em>I firmly believe</em>, <em>not only… but also</em>, <em>we owe them a lot</em>, <em>make a huge difference</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>

<Redaccion titulo="Redacción modelo · C2" idioma="en">
  <p>There are several people in my family I admire, but the one I admire most is my grandmother Carmen.</p>
  <p>She grew up in a small village in the 1950s, in a very poor family, and she had to leave school at the age of twelve to help her parents. In spite of this, she never stopped learning. She taught herself to read properly by borrowing books from a neighbour, and years later she opened a small shop that she ran for more than thirty years.</p>
  <p>What I admire most about her is her strength. She has had a hard life, but she is always positive and she never complains. Moreover, she is the most generous person I know: whenever someone in the family has a problem, she is the first one to help.</p>
  <p>She has also taught me that hard work pays off and that it is never too late to learn something new.</p>
  <p>In short, my grandmother is a real example for me, and I hope to be like her one day.</p>
  <p slot="comentario"><strong>Por qué funciona.</strong> Presenta a la persona, cuenta brevemente su historia con pasados (<em>grew up</em>, <em>had to leave</em>, <em>taught herself</em>) y luego explica por qué la admira, que es lo que pide la pregunta. Expresiones útiles: <em>the one I admire most</em>, <em>in spite of this</em>, <em>what I admire most about her is…</em>, <em>it is never too late to</em>.</p>
</Redaccion>
